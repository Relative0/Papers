## Existing contribution

- FJ1 (`thm:T2`, `thm:finiteC`): a nonzero finite jet forces a signed-parity orthant law.
- FJ2 (`thm:T3`, `thm:finitearr`): no-flat-point and uniform finite-order arrangement rigidity.
- FJ3 (`thm:T5`, `thm:T6`): normalized Roumieu realization dichotomies and exact counts.

These combine classical smooth signature-flatness and cutoff mechanisms with explicit finite-order formulations and a self-contained arrangement proof.

## Added consequences and attribution

- `prop:corner`: a zero k-jet plus a fixed Holder seminorm bounds a non-affine exact score by radius to the k+alpha power.
- `rem:associated`: a fixed Denjoy--Carleman derivative budget gives the standard associated-function margin bound.
- `thm:guardeddegree`: at fixed dimension, parity has degree equal to its support size; other laws have logarithmic degree in inverse excluded width; every nonconstant law has reciprocal-width degree under whole-cube amplitude and fixed margin. The proof reduces to rational approximation, Sherstov's majority-conjunction lower bound, and Bernstein--Jackson inequalities.
- `sec:rfunctions`: classical R-functions give exact finite-differentiability alternatives and sharp binary examples for the Lipschitz-derivative exponent.

The polynomial bounds fix dimension and margin. They establish no growing-arity, coefficient-bit, conditioning, circuit-size, learning, or CM-specific efficiency result. Spatial flatness is not a training-failure result.
