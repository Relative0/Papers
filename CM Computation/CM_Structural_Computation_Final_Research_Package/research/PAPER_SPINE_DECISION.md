# Paper spine decision and its recorded evolution

## Decision before Draft 1

**Endpoint:** preserve an exact bounded Boolean decomposition through repeated conditioning and exact scalar counting, then evaluate its complete setup/query contract.  
**Form:** methods / reproducibility technical report with elementary explanatory theory.  
**One-sentence contribution:** make the operational and assurance contracts of a small exact-decomposition portfolio explicit, test them against recovered source and independent scalar evaluation, and cost a repeated-query reference workload honestly.

Three contributions were supportable: (1) exact scopes and direct update/count rules; (2) executable source-contract observations; (3) a reproducible bounded experiment with negative outcomes retained. Generic decomposition novelty and competitive performance were not supportable.

**GO:** synthesize and improve a report. **NO-GO:** advertise a new CM computation theory, a first-certified Boolean representation, or industrial superiority.

## Changes after Round 1

Added profile counting, rank repair, fixed-cut bounds, actual binary storage, fused queries and legacy interoperability. The report became stronger, but neither novelty nor broad performance was established.

## Changes after Round 2

Added persistence counterexamples and source-access limits. Replaced a parse-only reload proxy with real fresh-instance replay for the final measurement. Repaired direct-constructor aliasing. The final spine remains operational correctness and evidence discipline, not a positive speedup thesis.

## Exclusions

FO calculus/lifting, CO pair rules/S-T-H/P14, phase/model results, guard synthesis, retrieval, learned routing and small operator atlases stay outside the contribution. Standard rank results are cited or proved as background. Missing industrial comparators remain open rather than being bypassed with a broader narrative.
