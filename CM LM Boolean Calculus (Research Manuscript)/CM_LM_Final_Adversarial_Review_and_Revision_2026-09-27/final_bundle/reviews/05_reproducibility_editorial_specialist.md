# Review 5 - Reproducibility and mathematical editorial specialist

**Review mode:** direct build, artifact inspection, checker execution, and submission-hygiene review.

## Initial recommendation
Not submission-ready until the finite-verification claim and several bibliography/process artifacts were repaired.

## Main findings
The pre-revision paper said that a checker accompanied the manuscript and referred to a 20,873-check history, but the generating artifact for that count was not recoverable in the supplied package. That is a reproducibility problem even though it does not refute the theorems.

Several internal-process phrases, a crowded figure label, an overpromising appendix title, and incomplete/fragile bibliography entries weakened submission hygiene.

## Revision response
Accepted. The paper now references a self-contained independent checker and machine-readable run record. A fresh execution passed 2,022,648 explicitly counted assertions and produced boundary-counterexample and compact-atlas files. The unsupported historical check-count claim has been removed from the manuscript. The source compiles to 32 pages with no unresolved citations/references and no overfull or underfull box warnings. The final PDF was rendered and visually inspected, including the new worked example, symbolic signed transport, related-work table, and verification appendix.

The title was sharpened to "Correspondence and Logical Matrices: A Typed Boolean Operator Calculus" to foreground the actual contribution.

## Post-revision recommendation
Pass for reproducibility and presentation, subject only to venue-specific front matter (affiliation, anonymization, ORCID, data/supplement policy) at submission time.
