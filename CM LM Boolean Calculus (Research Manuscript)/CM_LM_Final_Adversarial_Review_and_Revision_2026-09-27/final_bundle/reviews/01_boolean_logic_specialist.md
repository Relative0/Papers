# Review 1 - Boolean algebra and propositional logic specialist

**Review mode:** simulated independent specialist lens, applied directly to the pre-revision 31-page manuscript and then rechecked against the revision. This is not an external human referee report.

## Initial recommendation
Minor-to-moderate revision, with no central theorem withdrawal.

## Main findings
The binary frame normal form, valuation identities, agreement-bit pairing, pointwise Boolean lift, higher-arity formula lift, and the main coherence theorem are mathematically consistent under the declared Boolean semantics. I found no counterexample to the central results. The manuscript is strongest when it treats these identities as a typed representation calculus rather than as a new underlying Boolean algebra.

The higher-arity pairing proof was too compressed: it should explicitly identify the unique selected polarity index under a valuation and explain why the resulting cell contains exactly the agreement bits. The zero-rank case also needed an explicit empty-XOR convention.

## Revision response
Accepted. The higher-arity selector proof now identifies the selected tuple coordinate-by-coordinate; the zero-function case is stated with an empty XOR decomposition; empty flattening blocks are explicitly typed; and the abstract/intro now state that the constituent Boolean mechanisms are classical while the claimed contribution is their frame-aware integration.

## Post-revision recommendation
Pass on correctness and proof discipline. No remaining Boolean-semantics blocker found.
