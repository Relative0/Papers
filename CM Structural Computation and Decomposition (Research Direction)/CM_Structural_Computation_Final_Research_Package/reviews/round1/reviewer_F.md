**Status:** internal AI role-based adversarial review of frozen Draft 1. Not an external referee, separate model, or independently recruited reviewer.

Removing all CM terminology leaves a coherent question about exact representations and repeated scalar queries. That is a good sign for portfolio separation. It is not yet a strong argument that the article contains new science rather than a careful engineering notebook.

The best part is the distinction between four predicates: representing the right function, being compact, being minimal in a model, and being authenticated to an external source. These should be the organizing theme, not an implied competition with industrial KC. R1-F1: make the formal counterexamples and field-sensitive distinctions central. R1-F2: state which results are standard or elementary and which are genuinely new observations made in this pass. R1-F3: retain negative findings but do not title the work as a broad negative-results study from one tiny design.

A compelling failure test is whether all value disappears once JSON is replaced with a compact encoding. The semantic and trust-boundary results would survive; the storage selection result may not. Run that sensitivity, then organize the discussion around what survives.

I am not requesting extra unrelated topics. Containment lattices, NPN atlases, and retrieval would make the report longer without solving its contribution problem. A technical report with openly modest novelty is useful; a major-theorem article is unsupported. I would not treat lack of optimized baselines as a fatal defect for a contract audit, but it is fatal for a superiority claim.
