# Release notes: v0.8.1

Version 0.8.1 freezes the mathematically audited v0.8 manuscript after its
required minor expository revision. No theorem, proposition, corollary, proof,
or mathematical construction was replaced.

## Review basis

The release incorporates the earlier deep core audit and Astra review cycle,
the resulting v0.7-to-v0.8 editorial response, and a separate independent
referee-level audit of v0.8. The independent audit checked every formal claim,
all displayed calculations and figures, the cited precedent, and the known
failure modes involving indexed observations, incomplete transcripts,
pullbacks, non-T0 connectedness, the Dowker nerve, and contextuality.

That final audit found all 13 numbered theorem/proposition/corollary statements
valid under their stated assumptions and recommended only a minor revision for
publication as exposition. Its required revisions are incorporated here.

## Changes from v0.8

- Identified the intended reader and the concrete pedagogical payoff.
- Defined `Top` and clarified that `ProLT` names the topological space, while
  the indexed observations and weights are presentation data.
- Removed the potentially misleading aliases “logical point” and “truth point.”
- Replaced ambiguous “unfolding” and “truth topology” wording with standard
  terminology.
- Added precise source locators and an accessible Vickers reference.
- Declared the positive radius in the forward-ball definition.
- Corrected minor DNF punctuation and the early FCA example's forward reference.
- Reworded the nerve/homology warning as a comparison of homology groups and
  expanded the triangle example with its pairwise and triple intersections.

## Publication identity

Release v0.8.1 is suitable as an expository synthesis or pedagogical article.
The review record does not support presenting it as an original research result
without a separate new theorem, algorithm, invariant, or validated application.
