## Findings that change the research plan

- **The observation-dimension claim is invalid.** GR now records a correct predicate-cover formulation with a short proof. It is an elementary starting point, not an automatic hardness result. Safety under observation composition remains a meaningful constrained research question.
- **Several central CM/phase constructions have direct classical antecedents.** Numeric pointwise operator fusion, ANF/Möbius conversion, local/group algebra and much of the order-topological machinery need attribution and a narrower contribution statement. Useful exposition is still a valid output.
- **Ring support, primitive states, tensor products and process closure are different contracts.** P01’s nonprimitive resource separator cannot be compared with a primitive-state model without saying so. PC and BR are valuable because they make these differences explicit.
- **Compiler novelty is chiefly an implementation question.** A detailed historical audit recovered under SC limits the current implementation claims: root-only compilation, syntactic support and retained output axes matter. A matched endpoint and charged costs must precede any new speedup claim.
- **Current literature matters to proposed extensions.** The 2026 DSGRN and adaptive sparse Möbius preprints are relevant neighbors for GU and IP/SC. Their abstracts do not settle exact theorem priority, but they prevent treating those application areas as unexplored.

## What was checked

Current source and selected proof passages were read alongside the existing audits and local ownership evidence. Primary literature was inspected at the depth recorded in the literature ledger: some key theorem passages were read directly; some sources are abstract/metadata-level comparisons. The search is targeted and non-exhaustive. No “first ever” claim or submission guarantee is made.

Fresh independent checks passed:

- Direct support/global-assignment enumeration for five 2×2 Z/4 resources, using all 12 unordered projective bases and not using the Smith classification to decide support assignments.
- Equality of direct predicate-family and cover optima on 382 naturally labelled order pairs through four vertices.
- Two counterexamples to naive composition rules for observation-refinement homology, over F2.
- Boolean degree-q evaluation ranks in 35 cases through seven variables.
- Selected phase-ring zero-divisor/idempotent facts and four rational interior guard-region witnesses.

These are finite spot checks. They do not prove arbitrary-ring or arbitrary-size theorems, certify all collapse claims, or rerun the historical minimality/unitary/performance campaigns. No new performance benchmark was run.
