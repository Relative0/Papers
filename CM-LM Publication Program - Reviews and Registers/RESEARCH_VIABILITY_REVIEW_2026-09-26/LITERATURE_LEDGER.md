# Primary-source literature ledger

Access date: 2026-09-26. Depth varies; abstract-only entries are leads/comparators, not theorem-level exclusions. This ledger does not certify exhaustive priority.

### Rainer: Rainer — Ultradifferentiable extension theorems: a survey

[Rainer — Ultradifferentiable extension theorems: a survey](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.01061v2)

Inspection: Relevant full text: Theorem 3.6 and Corollary 3.12.

Relevance: Denjoy–Carleman quasianalyticity and cutoff existence are established inputs; compare total weight W_k=k!M_k.

### Orthants: Speyer and Wofsey — Characterizing orthants with polynomials (2014 original answers)

[Speyer and Wofsey — Characterizing orthants with polynomials (2014 original answers)](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/188631/characterizing-orthants-with-polynomials)

Inspection: Original arguments read; research forum, not a refereed journal.

Relevance: Polynomial orthant-sign parity follows from hyperplane factor multiplicities; close antecedent to FJ’s algebraic lemma.

### Monomial: Belotto da Silva and Bierstone — Monomialization of a quasianalytic morphism

[Belotto da Silva and Bierstone — Monomialization of a quasianalytic morphism](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.09502)

Inspection: Abstract and metadata.

Relevance: Modification-based monomialization is not literal unit factorization in the original coordinates; theorem-level comparison remains open.

### Forests: Bernardi and Klivans — Directed rooted forests in higher dimension

[Bernardi and Klivans — Directed rooted forests in higher dimension](https://arxiv.org/html/1512.07757v4)

Inspection: Relevant full-text discussion of fitting orientations and discrete vector fields.

Relevance: Rooted forests and matching language predate BT; a fitting orientation need not be an acyclic gradient matching.

### Quillen: Barmak — On Quillen’s Theorem A for posets

[Barmak — On Quillen’s Theorem A for posets](https://arxiv.org/html/1005.0538)

Inspection: Theorems 1.1–1.2 and comparable-map discussion.

Relevance: Contractible inverse images of principal ideals imply simple homotopy equivalence; this is not a literal collapse criterion.

### TU: Dey, Hirani and Krishnamoorthy — Optimal Homologous Cycles, Total Unimodularity, and Linear Programming

[Dey, Hirani and Krishnamoorthy — Optimal Homologous Cycles, Total Unimodularity, and Linear Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0338)

Inspection: Abstract/theorem synopsis.

Relevance: Boundary total unimodularity and relative torsion are established; BT must contribute the special incidence recognition.

### Ferrers: Dochtermann and Engström — Algebraic properties of edge ideals via combinatorial topology

[Dochtermann and Engström — Algebraic properties of edge ideals via combinatorial topology](https://www.combinatorics.org/Volume_16/PDF/v16i2r2.pdf)

Inspection: Full relevant text: Proposition 3.7 and its folding proof.

Relevance: The chain/Ferrers homotopy dichotomy is already credited prior art, not an independent BT novelty claim.

### MQT: Schumacher and Westmoreland — Modal quantum theory

[Schumacher and Westmoreland — Modal quantum theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.2929)

Inspection: Abstract and primary-record inspection.

Relevance: Finite-field modal entanglement, no-cloning and nonlocality are established.

### Almost: Schumacher and Westmoreland — Almost quantum theory

[Schumacher and Westmoreland — Almost quantum theory](https://arxiv.org/html/1204.0701)

Inspection: Relevant full-text sections on states/processes and probabilistic resolutions, including §5.3.

Relevance: Mixed subspace states and process semantics already exist; strong probabilistic resolution can fail.

### Sheaf: Abramsky and Brandenburger — The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality

[Abramsky and Brandenburger — The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264)

### Modk: de Beaudrap — On computation with probabilities modulo k

[de Beaudrap — On computation with probabilities modulo k](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.7381)

Relevance: Primitive/generic state constraints and uniform complexity differ from P01’s nonprimitive static resources and P02’s exact oracle-query contract.

### James: James, Ortiz and Sabry — Quantum Computing over Finite Fields

[James, Ortiz and Sabry — Quantum Computing over Finite Fields](https://arxiv.org/html/1101.3764v1)

Inspection: Relevant conclusion passage on Deutsch and reversible relational programming.

Relevance: Qualitative failure of the usual Deutsch algorithm is prior art; a fully quantified adaptive query lower bound is a narrower question.

### Diamond: Diamond and Schumacher — Cloning, deleting, and hiding in modal quantum theory
