**25 September 2026.** Comparison against the manuscripts in the seven selected publication folders, with the portfolio treated as background evidence. This report adds a targeted recovery check in Downloads and the three ZIP packages explicitly authorized during this task. It is a publication assessment and preservation map, not certification of novelty or of every proof.

## 1. Decisions in brief

**There is worthwhile material outside the selected papers, and some of it is already written as a paper. The immediate priority is to recover the existing manuscripts and revisions before commissioning more papers.**

1. **Two substantial research manuscripts are missing from the selected folder set:** *Contextuality and Exact Information Resources over Finite Chain Rings* (P01) and *Exact Query Bounds in Characteristic-Two Modal Computation* (P02). Both have PDFs, LaTeX and written general proofs. They survive in the excluded portfolio and CM research tree; they are not merely ideas awaiting a first draft. The selected phase papers do not replace their theorem packages.
2. **A materially revised computation manuscript survives only in the inspected Downloads material.** It is the 14-page post-split compiler draft, with explicit normalization/tabulation rules, S/T/H provenance and a scoped completeness theorem. The selected folders contain its older 24-page combined predecessor. The PDF added to `CM Computation` during this task is instead an exact copy of the LM-centered foundations paper.
3. **The seven folders currently represent eight distinct manuscript titles, not seven independent papers.** The modal folder contains both the signed/integral phase manuscript and the intrinsic Boolean phase manuscript; the foundations folder also contains the older computation manuscript. Counting folders, PDFs, or every historical working note as a paper would each give a misleading answer.
4. **The most promising additional theory project is general ProLT observation refinement**, especially refinements that split an existing observational class. Much of its starting material already exists in the v0.1/v0.2 notes recovered from Downloads. The current binary-order-thinning manuscript explicitly excludes that carrier-changing case. A coherent methods/theory article is plausible after proof and prior-art review; an omnibus collection of all old ProLT material is not recommended.
5. **Observation design is a plausible second research track, not a ready extra paper.** Recover the relative observation-dimension question and the joint-versus-sequential safety examples, repair the false 2-dimension identification, and connect restricted observation selection to the guarded-operator paper's existing minimum-test formulation. A new contribution would need a constrained theorem, algorithm, or convincing application beyond repackaging classical test-set/cover problems.
6. **Keep the full LM/modal bridge and process-closure material as a substantial companion or supplement.** Both contain useful proofs and negative results. Their own contribution boundaries, and the classical algebra underneath, argue against multiplying standalone research papers at present.
7. **A compact-CM spectral/dynamical atlas and an endpoint-matched computation study remain conditional opportunities.** The former is currently strongest as a reproducible reference or expository note; the latter requires traceable code, raw results and task-matched evaluation. Historical numerical claims in slides and summaries are not enough.

**Recommended publication load:** first resolve the eight current manuscript families and recover P01/P02; then develop **one** general-refinement project. Treat observation design as a staged follow-on or a component of that project until its independent contribution is demonstrated. Preserve other material without assuming it needs its own publication.

## 4. Existing manuscripts and revisions to recover first

### P01 — Chain-ring contextuality and exact resources: strongest existing overlooked research draft

**Recovered title:** *Contextuality and Exact Information Resources over Finite Chain Rings*. Mature 11-page PDF D086 and LaTeX D087; earlier six-page skeletons D214/D215 are historical precursors, not additional papers.

**Its central package:** a Smith-data trichotomy for complete-basis bipartite support; an exact orbit-coding count `d h`; an invertibility criterion for universal raw-vector teleportation; and nonprimitive resources separating maximal coding from teleportation. Here `h` is the multiplicity of the least Smith exponent. These are general written arguments under explicit contracts, not just the original small finite enumerations. [D087, L84–184](#source-d087)

**Why the selected papers do not absorb it:** S5's signed lift changes the scalar system; S6's intrinsic Boolean algebra and ANF pattern extraction are different tasks. Neither substitutes for the all-bases chain-ring classification, finite-local-ring analyzer construction and exact resource comparison. A reference to “teleportation” or “modal” is insufficient coverage.

**Recommendation:** restore P01 to the active publication register as an existing research draft. Keep its proofs together rather than splitting contextuality, coding and teleportation into small separate papers. The comparison among the resources is the coherent contribution.

**Before submission:** independently audit the all-basis quantifiers, nonprimitive state admissibility, teleportation branch argument and the `d h` upper bound; reconcile supporting data and bibliography; establish the nearest prior theorem under the same contracts. Nonzero ring vectors need not be closed under independent balanced tensoring, so retain the static-model qualification. The draft itself distinguishes de Beaudrap's unit-ideal state space from its own nonprimitive separators. [D087, L21–44](#source-d087)

The relevant background is substantial: field-modal entanglement and no-cloning already exist in [Schumacher–Westmoreland](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.2929), contextuality hierarchies in [Abramsky–Brandenburger](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264), and computation over scalar rings in [de Beaudrap](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.7381). My recommendation is based on the narrower classification/comparison package, not a claim that modal quantum theory itself is new.

### P02 — Exact characteristic-two query bounds: recover as a focused technical note

**Recovered title:** *Exact Query Bounds in Characteristic-Two Modal Computation: A technical note on oracle access, adaptive readout and kickback*. Nine-page PDF D098 and LaTeX D099. The earlier “Obstructions” skeleton D224/D225 is the same developing family.

**Written results:** exact Deutsch complexity two and exact Bernstein–Vazirani complexity `n` under standard XOR access; a one-query Deutsch–Jozsa obstruction under the broader fixed pointwise invertible-oracle contract; finite adaptive recorded-instrument proofs; and the distinction between ring-valued kickback and an invertible Fourier analyzer. **Why it is additional:** S6 can inspect a constructed ANF/pattern description; P02 forbids free coefficient inspection and charges oracle access. **Recommendation:** recover as an existing, self-contained technical note. Review the adaptive branch induction, exact-discrimination lemma and lower/upper-bound contract distinction. Do not inflate the small Simon scan into a general theorem. Further DJ/Simon research is optional; it should not delay preserving the current note.

Polynomial lower-bound methods already have a major antecedent in [Beals et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9802049), and the positive modal UNIQUE-SAT result is [Willcock–Sabry](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.3587). The draft credits these and explicitly withholds a firstness claim. [D099, L198–241](#source-d099)

## 5. Additional publication candidates, ranked without forcing a paper count

### R1 — General logical-observation refinement, including changes of carrier

**Disposition: develop one coherent theory/methods article; strongest residual candidate.** Starting drafts already exist: v0.1 *Homological Foundations* (X026/X027) and v0.2 *Observation Refinement* (X030/X031). They are working research notes, not two automatically independent submissions.

**Residual core:** a new observation can distinguish states previously identified by the old observation signature. The refined poset then has points `(p,a)` indexed by new signatures realized inside each old class, with a forgetful projection to `p`. This is not merely deletion of comparisons on a fixed vertex set. X031 gives the fiber-profile normal form, redundancy characterization, adjoint/fiber criteria and examples; X027 supplies mapping-cone and relative-homology machinery. [X031, L144–205](#source-x031) [X031, L377–614](#source-x031) [X027, L284–342](#source-x027)

**Why S8 does not cover it:** its ProLT interpretation expressly requires the new observation to factor through the current T0 quotient, and excludes carrier splitting. That is a deliberate scope boundary, not a lost paragraph that should simply be put back. [Z3-05, L915–947](#source-z3-05)

**Proposed focus:** *Observation Refinement Beyond a Fixed Carrier: Certificates and Defects in Finite Logical Models*. The title is a working proposal. Use fiber profiles to connect logical conditions to computable certificates; distinguish redundancy, homology preservation, homotopy preservation and sufficient fiber tests. Include examples separating those notions and a practical certificate-producing implementation or a new restricted-class theorem.

**Existing material versus research still required:**

- Existing: explicit normal form, forgetful map, mapping-cone construction, possible/forced-truth criteria and counterexamples to necessity of fiber tests.
- Audit required: every quantifier in the adjoint criteria; finite-space weak equivalence versus order-complex homotopy equivalence; coefficient conventions; the unrestricted undecidability reduction's exact hypotheses.
- Potential new contribution: a useful logically defined family where certificates are complete or efficiently computable; alternatively a well-tested tool that returns a preservation certificate or an explicit homological defect.
- Evidence gate: reproduce the small separating examples and demonstrate an example that the fixed-carrier theorem cannot handle.

[Barmak's poset form of Quillen's Theorem A](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.0538) already supplies strong fiber criteria. Rewording that theorem with “ProLT” terminology is not enough. **Stop or demote to a methods chapter** if the new work remains only textbook finite-poset topology in signature notation. Preserve the existing notes even in that outcome.

### R2 — Observation selection with semantic and topological constraints

**Disposition: promising research program, not submission-ready; combine with R1 initially unless a distinct theorem/algorithm emerges.** Sources are the residual v0.5 material X033 and S4's existing restricted-dispatch section.

There are three distinct design tasks:

1. Identify a realized Boolean/CM operator using a restricted set of score signs or predicates. S4 already treats this through minimum test/predicate sets.
2. Realize a target same-carrier order thinning with as few new Boolean observations as possible. v0.5 proposes relative observation dimension; its identification with classical Boolean-lattice embedding dimension is false.
3. Select or schedule observations while preserving a chosen logical/topological property. This adds a constraint absent from generic operator identification, and the old notes contain useful interaction counterexamples.

**Do not revive the false result.** X033 defines `odim_P(P)=0` but later identifies `odim_P(Q)` with classical 2-dimension when P is a linear extension of Q. Taking P=Q to be a two-element chain already refutes that equality: no observations are needed to preserve the order, but embedding two distinct points needs a nonzero-dimensional Boolean lattice. The dependent hardness proof therefore does not establish its claim. A different valid hardness theorem is not ruled out. The current S8 changelog correctly removes both assertions. [X033, L213–270](#source-x033) [Z3-31, L151–161](#source-z3-31)

**A concrete repair direction:** model a bit as a Q-upset that separates selected deleted P-comparisons. Seek a minimum family of such bits, or a restricted allowed-formula library, that realizes the target relation; then impose the required preservation certificates. This is a proposed formulation for the next research stage, not a newly certified priority claim. Keep “unrestricted abstract bit,” “permitted propositional formula,” “weighted predicate,” and “adaptive probe” as different cost models.

**Important residual examples:** the three-chain profiles `010` and `101` are individually homotopy-neutral but jointly disconnect the complex. A five-state example has the converse scheduling difficulty: neither coordinate is a safe first step, yet the joint block collapses to the target. Both examples are written in X033 and are not explicitly developed in S8. [X033, L402–494](#source-x033)

Independent checks here reproduce their F2 Betti vectors: three-state case `(1,0,0)` for the coarse and each individual refinement, versus `(2,0,0)` jointly; five-state case `(1,0,0)` coarse/joint versus `(1,1,0)` for each individual step, with higher entries zero. These checks support the examples' homology statements; they are not a general proof that homology preservation implies homotopy preservation.

**Possible publishable increment:** a theorem or algorithm that characterizes/optimizes safe observation batches for a meaningful class, with lower bounds, certificates and comparison to unconstrained selection. A small family of corrected examples alone belongs in a supplement. Classical minimum test-set work already studies separating objects with a smallest collection of tests ([Berman–DasGupta–Kao](https://www.cs.uic.edu/~dasgupta/resume/publ/papers/ber3-swat04.pdf)); S4 already acknowledges that boundary. [Z2-04, L380–479](#source-z2-04)

## Appendix C. Primary literature checked for positioning

These sources establish close antecedents, not an exhaustive absence-of-prior-art conclusion. The local manuscripts' more extensive bibliographies remain part of their source packages.

- [Schumacher and Westmoreland, *Modal Quantum Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.2929) — existing field-modal entanglement and no-cloning background.
- [Schumacher and Westmoreland, *Almost Quantum Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.0701) — mixed states, generalized measurements and open dynamics.
- [Abramsky and Brandenburger, *The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264) — established support/global-section framework.
- [de Beaudrap, *On computation with probabilities modulo k*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.7381) — computational models over semirings/rings; a different question from the exact P02 oracle promise.
- [Willcock and Sabry, *Solving UNIQUE-SAT in a Modal Quantum Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.3587) — known positive modal-query example.
- [Beals et al., *Quantum Lower Bounds by Polynomials*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9802049) — polynomial-method antecedent.
- [Barmak, *On Quillen's Theorem A for posets*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.0538) — fiber criteria and simple-homotopy refinement of established results.
- [Bernardi and Klivans, *Directed Rooted Forests in Higher Dimension*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.07757) — established rooted-forest/orientation machinery relevant to S8 and the old block notes.
- [Toffano, *Eigenlogic in the spirit of George Boole*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.06632) — spectral logical operators; distinguish from native compact-CM semantics.
- [Berman, DasGupta and Kao, *Tight Approximability Results for Test Set Problems in Bioinformatics*](https://www.cs.uic.edu/~dasgupta/resume/publ/papers/ber3-swat04.pdf) — established minimum separating-test problems.
- [Cummins et al., *Combinatorial Representation of Parameter Space for Switching Networks*](https://chomp.rutgers.edu/papers/DSGRN_paper.pdf) — established parameter-to-logic/dynamics methods.
