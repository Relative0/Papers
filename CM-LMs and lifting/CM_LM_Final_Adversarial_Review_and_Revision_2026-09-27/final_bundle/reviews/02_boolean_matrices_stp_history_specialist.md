## Main findings
The raw same-frame numerical superposition law is not a plausible novelty claim: Cheng, Zhao, and Xu (2011), Proposition 3.3, explicitly state the corresponding entrywise law for truth tables under an arbitrary binary logical operator. The paper already moved in this direction, but the comparison should be direct.

A close frame antecedent is Gudder and Latremoliere's Boolean inner-product-space construction: their cyclic basis generated from a stochastic vector gives, in the two-dimensional case, the same complementary-row mechanism that underlies the symbolic frame matrix. This does not duplicate the entire CM/LM calculus, but it materially limits frame-level priority claims.

One genuine notation bug was found in the STP comparison: the manuscript used a row truth vector where its own vec convention defines a column vector.
