# Hostile Review A - Novelty and priority attack

**Mandate:** assume the least charitable technically informed reading and try to reduce every claimed contribution to known machinery.

## Attack
A skeptical referee can say: the numeric objects are reshaped truth tables; pointwise Boolean superposition is classical; the frame matrix is a two-dimensional instance of a known Boolean-space basis/reflection mechanism; signed input changes are NPN-style actions; higher-arity tensors are truth tensors; conjunctive XOR decomposition is ordinary F2 rank factorization; ANF and spectral material are standard coordinate changes. Therefore, the paper must not imply that these ingredients are new simply because they are unified under CM/LM terminology.

The attack is strongest against theorem-level priority, not against correctness.

## What survived the attack
No exact duplicate of the full package - formula-valued polarity lift, agreement-bit pairing, explicit frame typing, symbolic/numeric transport, and one coherence statement tying those operations together - was located in the targeted search. That absence is not proof of priority. The paper can still make a defensible contribution as a representation/specification synthesis if it is explicit that many mechanisms are classical.

## Required changes accepted
- Reframe the contribution as architectural/structural synthesis.
- Credit the exact 2011 entrywise law instead of treating it as new.
- Compare the two-dimensional Boolean frame mechanism directly with Gudder-Latremoliere.
- Add a modern matrix-product comparison.
- Remove rhetoric suggesting a new Boolean algebra, quantum measurement semantics, compression, or speedup.
- Make the title and opening page emphasize the typed calculus.

## Post-revision hostile verdict
The paper is still not a strong candidate if sold as a major new theorem paper. The attack no longer supports rejection for overclaiming if the current restrained framing is retained. The novelty ceiling remains the main reason the readiness score is not in the 9+/10 range overall.
