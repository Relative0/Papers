# Venue-specific submission checklist

The mathematical/content review is complete. Before journal upload, check only venue-dependent items:

- Apply the journal's LaTeX/template and reference style.
- Add author affiliation, email, ORCID, and funding/conflict statements if required.
- Anonymize author/self-identifying supplement information if the venue is double blind.
- Add MSC/ACM classifications if requested.
- Verify abstract/keyword/page limits.
- Upload `independent_verify.py`, `verification_results.json`, `boundary_counterexamples.json`, and `compact_cm_atlas.json` under the venue's supplement/data policy.
- In the cover letter, describe the work as a **typed Boolean representation calculus/synthesis**, and explicitly acknowledge the closest antecedents rather than claiming ingredient-level firstness.
- Do not make compression, speedup, or physical-measurement claims unless separate evidence is supplied.
