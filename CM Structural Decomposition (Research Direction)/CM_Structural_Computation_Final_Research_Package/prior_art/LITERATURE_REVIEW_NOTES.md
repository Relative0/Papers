# Literature comparison and limits

The machine-readable matrix contains exact source URLs and inspection depth. The review searched more broadly than the final paper; a search hit is not treated as a verified theorem or priority clearance.

## Load-bearing comparisons

**Knowledge compilation and BDDs (P01--P02):** support the query/representation lens. The report cannot originate this paradigm. Its candidate distinction is the actual small-portfolio operational audit and evidence, not 'compile once'.

**DSD and ACD (P03--P05):** make generic Boolean decomposition and cofactor organization established prior art. A whole-row prototype dictionary is not the same representation as a structurally encoded ACD selector. Don't-care optimizations have different semantics from unrestricted exact counting of a fixed total function.

**Tensor representations and counting (P06, P11):** block a generic first factorized/tensor Boolean-counting claim. The report's GF(2) field matters: ordinary arithmetic tensor contraction and parity matrix multiplication cannot be interchanged. The fixed-cut example is proved directly, without making a lower bound against all tensor orders.

**Certified KC (P07):** is stronger in assurance than the tested scalar Python checker here. The latter avoids sharing the producer evaluator and takes an external truth source, but neither that separation nor a hash is formal verification.

**Rank notions, transforms and algebraic counting (P08--P10):** motivate careful distinctions, not claimed new primitives. Standard character identities and rank factorizations remain standard even when they serve a new audit.

## Late primary-source checks

The 2026 sparse-transform work (P13) uses a different arithmetic/oracle model; it is preserved as a research connection but not imported. Xia's September 2026 tensor-network framework (P14) concerns signature-set network counting and was only abstract-triaged. Its title is not evidence that every dichotomy is resolved, and it does not supply a directly implemented comparison for these listed GF(2) matrices.

Broad searches also surfaced verifiable SQL-query engines and unrelated neural tensor operations. These were excluded as mismatched endpoints, not silently counted as supporting literature.

## Negative novelty conclusion

No historical-first status is assigned to the closure/count identities, rank repair, quotient-space bound or elementary query limits. There may be a useful integration/methodological contribution, but the current external search does not certify its novelty. The strongest supported source of originality in this pass is the concrete audited implementation, new reproducible code and data, and the corrections exposed by the two revision cycles.
