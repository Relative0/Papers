# Deferred directions, not lost results

## Potential continuation of this report

**Broader optimized evaluation.** Use more independent external designs, a justified local-support range, optimized packed/BDD/ACD/DSD or KC comparators and measured memory. Freeze a heldout design split before looking at outcomes. The gate is a repeatable space/query regime after actual discovery and checking, not one favorable synthetic function.

**Query-family-specific persistent summaries.** The final histogram counterexample shows that unrestricted coordinate queries require assignment information. A restricted query family may admit a smaller state. A useful next theorem would characterize exactly what information can be quotiented without changing that family of counts. This is a research proposal, not a proved novelty claim; marginal/Fourier and database-view prior art must be checked.

**Sparse/oracle discovery.** Avoiding full-table discovery could change economics, but changes the source access model. The 2026 sparse real-valued Mobius-learning paper is a nearby comparator, not an automatic GF(2) implementation. A new project would need explicit oracle arithmetic and query costs.

**Care-set and weighted counting.** Existing ACD work uses don't-care freedom. For exact counting, altering unconstrained outputs changes the total unless the counted domain and weights are specified. This may yield a coherent extension, but is not implemented here and must not duplicate the guard-synthesis paper.

## Separate possible papers

**Structural retrieval:** only after defining an independently useful target, such as optimization behavior across unrelated circuit histories. Mutation-derived family labels and syntactic fingerprints alone are circular evidence. No retrieval result was executed in this pass.

**Selector/routing study:** require low-cost oracle headroom before learning or elaborate selection. Historical reported speedups remain unreconciled, and P14 belongs to the compiler paper.

**Atlas:** support containment, tiny operator orbits and A-Phi-B decompositions may be educational or software-reference appendices. They are not a standalone novelty core here.

**General tensor-network complexity:** the 2026 Xia framework studies fixed signature sets and complex-valued network contraction. Its input language is not these explicit GF(2) factor tables. It was triaged, not fully theorem-audited or incorporated.

**Modal/phase/chain-ring connections:** explicitly outside this paper; those are owned by separate research projects. A resemblance in 2x2 notation does not establish a useful bridge.
