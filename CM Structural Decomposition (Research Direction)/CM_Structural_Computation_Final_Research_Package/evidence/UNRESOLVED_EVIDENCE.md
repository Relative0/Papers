Labels such as 'fresh run' in the old archive identify historical snapshots, not new runs performed for this report. Historical C35/C36/P14 campaigns were not fully rerun with their original environment and backend configuration. Their numerical summaries remain historical evidence only.

No optimized CUDD baseline was obtained in the runtime; failure logs are retained. Other optimized baselines were not substituted by invented numbers. No independent reviewer or model was called. No external multi-design prevalence or heldout study was performed. These omissions limit the publication form, not the truth of the narrower source and finite-test observations.

The pinned EPFL source was retrieved as text because direct container networking failed; reconstructing the original line breaks produced the exact archived SHA-256. Both that source and its MIT license are included. This is materially stronger provenance than merely quoting a web filename.
