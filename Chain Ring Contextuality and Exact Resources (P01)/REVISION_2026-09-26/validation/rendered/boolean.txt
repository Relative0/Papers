This note records an optional symbolic construction. The main article does not depend on it. It
supplies neither a physical probability model nor a preparation theory closed under all conditioning
operations.
1 Scalar enrichment and support
Let B be the Boolean ring of formulas modulo logical equivalence and letA = F2[u]/(u4). Every

if e(1−e) = 0, one ofe and 1−e is a unit. A unital mapB→A therefore cannot generateu.

of bare Boolean formulas and does not automatically extend to mixed-characteristic scalar rings.
