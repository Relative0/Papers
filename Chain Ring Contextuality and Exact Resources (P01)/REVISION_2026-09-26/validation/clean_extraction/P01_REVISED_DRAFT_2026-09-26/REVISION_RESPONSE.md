## Additional explanatory consequence

Corollary 4.6 states and proves that, for primitive square resources, maximal d^2 coding, invertibility and universal exact raw teleportation coincide. This is an immediate consequence of the retained results, not a new independent novelty claim. The maximal-coding/nonteleportation separator is explicitly nonprimitive. Strong contextuality alone still does not force teleportation when 2<=h<d.
