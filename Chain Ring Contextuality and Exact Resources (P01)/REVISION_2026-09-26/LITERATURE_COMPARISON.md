# Source comparison for the revised P01 draft

Checked 26 September 2026. This is a bounded comparison supporting the article's scope and attribution, not a certificate of first publication. Source titles in the bibliography retain stable citation keys even when their publication years were updated.

## Closest contracts

| Source | Domain and admissibility | Settings / transformations / readout | Exactness and conclusion compared with P01 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schumacher-Westmoreland, Modal Quantum Theory | Finite fields; nonzero vectors | Reversible linear transformations, tensor composites and basis effects; possibility from nonzero contractions | Modal communication and nonlocality precedents. P01's target is the chain-ring Smith classification and fixed-resource dh optimization, not the first existence of modal coding or teleportation. |
| Abramsky-Brandenburger | General empirical models and measurement covers | Compatible outcome assignments and global sections | Supplies the hierarchy and obstruction language. P01 specifies a complete-basis family and classifies the resulting support tables. |
| de Beaudrap, Definition 7 and Proposition 2 | Generic ring states have coordinate ideal equal to the whole ring; Galois-ring transformation results | Generic-state transformations are left-invertible in the stated Galois-ring setting; circuits use preparations and bounded-arity gates, with outcomes interpreted computationally | P01 permits all nonzero static resources. Its nonprimitive separator is excluded by generic-state admissibility. Its fixed-resource communication task is different from circuit-complexity classification. |
| Honold-Landjev; Byrne et al. | Chain-ring modules, linear codes and their types | Semilinear code equivalence, finite geometry, module enumeration/density | Classical algebraic and coding foundations; these source comparisons do not establish an identical basis-support or raw-transfer theorem. |
| Zelinsky; Henriksen | Linear transformations / matrix rings and their units | Additive decomposition into nonsingular maps or units | Stronger antecedents to matrix spanning. P01 uses the elementary span argument and local lifting as tools, not independent novelty. |
| Feng-Nobrega-Kschischang-Silva | Chain-ring matrices in prescribed ambient modules | Stochastic transfer and errors in Y=AX+BE; decoding from the channel output | Capacity under specified distributions. P01 controls U in UM and requires disjoint support after one complete joint decoder. The uniform-square-transfer comparison in the introduction is explicitly P01's inference from the channel definition. |
| Varughese, Examples 8.3-8.4 | Categorical finite-field protocols | The qutrit example inspected by the supplied audit uses three Bell outcomes on a nine-dimensional pair | A protocol precedent under a different measurement contract; P01's analyzer consists of a full basis of d^2 covectors, and recovery is raw and reference-preserving. |
| Ben-Zvi-Ma-Reyes | Integer/ring matrix idempotents | Compatible-idempotent colorings | A ring-valued KS obstruction; not the same problem as a fixed bipartite resource with basis-indexed choices. P01 does not impose extra equality of truth values for rays repeated across bases. |

The added primitive-resource corollary is derived inside P01 from the retained theorems. It does not rely on a claim that these sources prove or disprove the separator.

## Priority conclusion

The revised contribution is the complete chain-ring classification and the comparison under explicit task contracts. The manuscript credits established matrix spanning, module theory, modal protocols and contextuality language. Neither the received audit's bounded negative search nor this targeted verification proves historical uniqueness.
