# Publication-stage scorecard — 30 September 2026

This is an editorial **readiness** estimate for each item's most defensible publication form, not a probability of acceptance, a claim of originality, or a new mathematical referee report. I used the collection's current artifacts, revision records, recent gate decisions, and the latest available Structured Factorization campaign. The numerical judgments below are mine; they are deliberately rounded to half-points rather than copied from incompatible weighted review scorecards. AI review and finite checks do not substitute for human specialist review. A score of 10 would require a settled venue/form, author-approved metadata and disclosures, and a final release packet; none is documented here.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9–9.5 | Scientifically reviewed submission candidate; mainly author, venue, archive or specialist-priority finishing work. |
| 7–8.5 | Coherent manuscript or reviewed companion; bounded editorial, positioning or review gates remain. |
| 5–6.5 | Useful draft/report or verified evidence; substantive integration or a publication-identity decision remains. |
| 3–4.5 | Research result or theorem note without a current integrated submission manuscript. |
| 0–2.5 | Exploratory direction or preliminary notes. |
