## Cross-program boundaries

FO → CO is foundations to compiler, not a shared manuscript. SC conditioning/decomposition Draft 3 is a separate methods report; the later T3 handoff is unintegrated evidence. P01, P02, PC, SP and IP use different scalar, resource, oracle or readout contracts. BR and AU record why those contracts cannot be collapsed. PL → BT → GR traces framework, fixed-carrier sharp theorem and general refinement research; The MIND owns the later integrated refinement paper and shared measurement work. Paper A studies sign realization under regularity; Paper B studies dispatch under specified guards. RG and website deliveries are not papers.

The historical “To publish” list remains useful evidence of intended releases but does not override later technical-companion decisions for IP/SP or identify a newer SC manuscript.
