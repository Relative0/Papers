Original delivery ZIPs were retained unchanged. The September 30 [creation receipt](<REVISION_ARCHIVES_2026-09-30.json>) records 12 additional revision snapshots. [Verification results](<OTHER_PAPERS_CLEANUP_VERIFICATION_2026-10-01.json>) include ZIP checks, hashes and reconciliation counts.

These ZIPs are **separate revision snapshots**, not new paper identities. The 23 covered older loose folders, the two older Structural Computation draft folders already exactly covered by ZIPs, and the four older root manuscript files were retired after hash and link checks. Current manuscripts, distinct review and reproducibility evidence, historical source collections and active workspaces remain unpacked where their roles require it. Some historical scripts or frozen navigation copies still refer to the old layouts and require extraction into a staging copy before rerunning.

The workspace `.gitignore` excludes `*.zip`, including these new archives and the original delivery ZIPs. They are local filesystem artifacts with hash receipts, not Git-tracked backups.
