# Optional Cover-Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **"Finite-Jet Sign Rigidity on Hyperplane Arrangements and Denjoy--Carleman Realization"** for publication as a short mathematical note.

The paper studies sign labelings on orthants and chambers of finite real affine hyperplane arrangements. The underlying smooth sign-rigidity phenomenon belongs to the established signature-flatness theory of Broglia and Tognoli, and the manuscript states this relationship explicitly rather than claiming historical firstness. The contribution is a self-contained leading-jet treatment that isolates explicit finite-order C^d and C^N rigidity statements and combines them with the classical Denjoy--Carleman quasianalyticity criterion and constructive nonquasianalytic selectors. This yields exact realization classifications: for an arrangement of m distinct hyperplanes with r chambers, the full normalized Roumieu class realizes exactly 2^(m+1) signed-parity laws in the quasianalytic case and all 2^r chamber labelings in the nonquasianalytic case.

I believe the note may be of interest to readers working in real analysis, quasianalytic/ultradifferentiable classes, and hyperplane arrangements because it gives a compact common formulation of the rigidity and realization sides without requiring division, resolution, or monomialization machinery.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Brian Theory
