## 1. Binary Order Thinning

**Applicable review:** 27 September 2026 publication-readiness audit of `binary_order_thinning_finite_posets_reviewed_v3.tex` [B1]. Its exact revised PDF matches the original website copy.

**Recorded mathematical status:** scientific GO for a focused research note/article after ordinary submission preparation. The reviewer found no unresolved correctness blocker after reconstructing the one-bit theorem, seven-state two-bit counterexample, computer-assisted minimality claim and cone-whisker construction. A real proof-writing gap was repaired: full row rank alone does not imply zero integral cokernel; the revised proof supplies a spanning-tree minor of determinant plus or minus one.

**Completeness and evidence:** the report records passing one-bit assertions, an exact seven-state check, and exhaustive searches finding no counterexample for sizes 1 through 6. For size 6 it records 4,117 naturally labelled height-two posets, 16,863,232 two-bit profiles and 3,767,044 F2-neutral cases. These are historical review results, not new reruns in this audit. The lower-bound proof now explicitly explains why searching the larger F2-neutral class is sufficient. Minimality remains computer-assisted, while the main structural claims have analytic proofs.

**Prior art and novelty:** rooted incidence, discrete Morse theory and the rooted-forest/collapse mechanism are standard. The report discusses Bernardi-Klivans, Mukherjee (2018/2020), Contreras-Tawfeek (2024), Forman and Kozlov, and distinguishes the Boolean construction of Bjoerner-Goresky-MacPherson (2024). The proposed contribution is the Boolean height-two thinning rule forcing the incidence form, plus sharp bit-count and height failure regimes. Exact theorem priority remains unconfirmed. The older viability decision [B2] already supported focused research, but its v2-era next gate should not be mistaken for an unresolved v3 proof defect.

**Items retained for later reference:** persistent code/log archive and citation; venue-specific affiliation/contact/ORCID/classification requirements; external specialist judgment on theorem priority and the minimality certificate. The latest report does not call for another broad internal rewrite. Do not extend one-bit, height-two, same-carrier or direct-collapse conclusions beyond their stated scope.
