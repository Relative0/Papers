## The central distinction

An ultrametric hierarchy organizes states by degrees of similarity. Similarity alone does not make two different states indistinguishable: an ultrametric is still a metric. A restricted **observation map** is what groups states that an observer cannot tell apart.

## What the note does

The MIND note gives a precise account of finite ball partitions, observation quotients, resolution towers, and an adaptive refinement index for a regular rooted tree. It also separates symmetry indices from information gain. Much of the underlying mathematics is established background; the note is framed as a correction and synthesis.

## Why it matters to The MIND

Concepts often depend on resolution. At one level, many states receive the same description; a finer observation separates them. This page connects the historical ultrametric and unconsciousness theme to the more explicit observation language of [ProLTs](/logical-topology/) and [concept differentiation](/observation-refinement/).

## Current boundary

The note does not formalize a complete psychology of unconscious thought or establish an empirical model of consciousness. Its claim is about mathematical observability in a specified hierarchy.
