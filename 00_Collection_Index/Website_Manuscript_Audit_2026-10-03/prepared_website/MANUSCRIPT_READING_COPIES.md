## Version convention

Reading-copy numbers identify fixed PDF bytes selected for this website. They are separate from manuscript revision numbers and do not imply journal acceptance or human peer review. Copy 2 records only author/metadata corrections. Superseded copy-1 PDFs have been moved out of the active public manuscript directory to `manuscript-history/reading-copy-1/`; their old public filenames are retired. Original scientific sources and review packages remain preserved in the publication collection.
