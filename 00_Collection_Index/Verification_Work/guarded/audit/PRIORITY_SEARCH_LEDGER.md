# Focused priority and literature-search ledger

Audit date: 28 September 2026. Primary papers, author manuscripts, publisher records, and arXiv records were used for substantive comparisons. This is a finite search with selective theorem-level reading, not a systematic review or proof of absence of prior art. Duplicate source copies were not treated as corroborating independent research.

## Exact comparison targets

The target of the search is not whether continuous-to-Boolean abstraction, sign partitions, minimum test sets, or polynomial descriptions have existed before; they have. The narrower uncertain question is whether the exact fixed-degree, fixed-original-coordinate four-label dispatch threshold for the signed-magnitude model F(x,y)=x+y+xy has already been stated or follows so directly from a prior result that it should be presented entirely as a worked corollary.

The revised manuscript makes no first-ever assertion for this calculation. Its correctness is established independently of its novelty. Its strongest honest positioning is a complete methods example with explicit observation and certification contracts.

## Access and inference boundaries

Moret and Shapiro's accessible publisher abstract is enough to establish the pre-existing objective; detailed historical algorithm claims were not inferred from inaccessible text. The Berman author manuscript provides the exact pair-cover formulation. The published DSGRN PDF first page and introduction verify its title, DOI, parameter partition, and dynamical scope. Current arXiv entries were checked directly and remain labelled preprints.

The new polynomial-description citation uses the verified arXiv record rather than claiming publisher metadata had been checked when access failed. Publisher-access failures are not evidence against the underlying papers. None of the consulted sources was shown to contain the exact m_d statement for this specific four-operator model, but this does not establish originality or adequate publication significance.

**Outcome:** no broad novelty claims approved. Concrete theorem correct under its stated contract; theorem-level priority unresolved. The paper is positioned as an elementary, exact, self-contained worked methods analysis.
