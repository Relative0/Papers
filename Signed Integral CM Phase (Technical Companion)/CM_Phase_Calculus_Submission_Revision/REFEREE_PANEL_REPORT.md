# Referee Panel and Adversarial Review Report

**Manuscript reviewed:** *Signed and Integral Lifts of Correspondence Matrices: Phase quotients, Hamming-isometry no-go results, and exact circuit encodings*  
**Review date:** 27 September 2026  
**Important status note:** These are independent **role-based specialist referee passes performed by the AI**, not reports from external human referees. Each pass was run with a different technical brief and the synthesis accepted only criticisms that could be supported by a proof check, computation, or literature evidence.

## Executive assessment

The broad pre-revision framing would have been vulnerable to rejection for overstatement of novelty: group-ring rotation, the quotient to Gaussian integers, exact Clifford+T arithmetic, and Boolean/sum-over-paths semantics all have substantial prior art. The mathematics that remains after claim discipline is substantially stronger. I found no fatal correctness error in the revised core results. The finite classifications rerun cleanly, and an independent reimplementation reproduces the critical counts and identities.

The paper is defensible as a correction-oriented technical companion / focused mathematical-computational note. It is **not** defensible as a paper claiming a new quantum theory, a new exact-synthesis algorithm, or a general simulation speedup.

## Referee 1 — finite rings, coding theory, and Hamming isometries

### Main attack
The strongest initial objection is that the “32 global isometries” result looks more novel than it is. Once the coefficient weight is recognized as ordinary binary Hamming weight on 4d coordinates, a weight-preserving linear map is a coordinate permutation. Requiring F2[C4]-linearity forces that permutation to commute with d disjoint 4-cycles, yielding the wreath product C4 wr S_d. The d=2 count 32 is therefore a short specialization of classical Hamming-isometry structure, not a new general MacWilliams theorem.

The classical context is MacWilliams extension theory and its finite-ring generalizations, including J. A. Wood, *American Journal of Mathematics* 121 (1999), DOI 10.1353/ajm.1999.0024, and M. Greferath & S. E. Schmidt, *JCTA* 92 (2000), DOI 10.1006/jcta.1999.3033.

### Correctness findings
- The proof of the global coefficient-Hamming classification is valid for the stated coefficient weight.
- The proof correctly distinguishes coefficient Hamming weight from standard Hamming weight on ring symbols.
- The shell count 512, 128 distinct shell actions, and four-element action kernel are reproduced computationally.
- The kernel explanation via the even-parity span is coherent and matches the independent check.
- The no-fringe conclusion is properly restricted to the specified shell/operator/interferometer universe.

### Required revision
Accepted and implemented: explicitly position the theorem beside MacWilliams/Wood/Greferath-Schmidt and state that its contribution is a specialized CM classification rather than a new general isometry theorem.

### Recommendation
**Minor revision / accept as a component of a technical note.** It is correct but not by itself a novelty centerpiece.

## Referee 2 — algebra and representation theory

### Main attack
The paper risks conflating four distinct facts: a C4 permutation action, the group algebra F2[C4], an integral lift Z[C4], and the quotient Z[C4]/(1+g^2) ≅ Z[i]. If those transitions are not typed explicitly, the argument can accidentally “derive” signed complex arithmetic from characteristic-two XOR.

### Correctness findings
- F2[C4] ≅ F2[g]/((g+1)^4) is correct in characteristic two.
- QP = JQ is verified directly and independently.
- ker(Q) is exactly the ideal generated by 1+g^2 in Z[C4].
- The carry formula Q(A XOR B) = QA + QB - 2Q(A AND B) is exact.
- Q^T Q = I - P^2 and the phase seminorm formula are correct.
- The conditional uniqueness theorem for the quotient quadratic form is correct given its explicit assumptions; it should not be sold as a derivation of physical probability.
- The raw CM Hadamard construction is valid only after the scalar change to integer path counts and quotienting; the revised text states this.

### Required revision
Accepted and implemented: sharpen the wording that the Gaussian quotient is standard algebra and the contribution is the explicit CM-coordinate intertwiner and semantic boundary, not discovery of Z[i].

### Recommendation
**Pass.** The revised scalar-domain discipline is one of the manuscript’s strongest features.

## Referee 3 — quantum information, exact synthesis, and path-sum methods

### Main attack
The initial framing risked presenting standard quantum arithmetic as a CM discovery. Exact Clifford+T coefficient rings and Boolean/sum-over-paths semantics are established. The circuit section must be validation of a translation, not the novelty claim.

### Correctness findings
- The exact Clifford+T arithmetic is consistent with established coefficient-domain results.
- The path-sum expression is a specialization of known Boolean/sum-over-paths formulations.
- The phase-oracle XOR correction is correct as an integer phase identity.
- Exact CHSH conjugations were independently checked; the observables are implemented with Clifford+T gates and the claimed 2 sqrt(2) value is consistent.
- The paper correctly states that these are centralized exact calculations, not experimental Bell tests and not evidence of a physical nonlocal Boolean device.
- The measurement/postselection caveat is appropriately separated from unitary closure.

### Required revision
Accepted and implemented: downgrade “exactness” and “path” results from novelty-signaling theorems to propositions, cite established exact-synthesis and path-sum literature, and say explicitly that they validate the CM translation.

### Recommendation
**Pass for a technical companion; reject the same material if marketed as a new quantum computational formalism.**

## Referee 4 — reproducibility and research software

### Reproduction performed
The primary programs were rerun from the revision package:

- `verify_finite.py`: complete 65,536-operator search; 32 global coefficient-Hamming isometries; 512 shell preservers; zero target fringe hits.
- `verify_quantum.py`: 240 exact random circuits; 5,760 exact normalization checks; 120 independent exact path/state comparisons; Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover, Bell/CHSH, and 8,192 predicate-phase checks.
- `verify_bridges.py`: pointwise-CM, convolution, rank, oracle, and shell-kernel bridge checks.
- `independent_hostile_checks.py`: separate reimplementation of QP=JQ, carry, global/shell counts, phase profiles, no-fringe result, and oracle identities without importing the project backend.

All reruns passed.

### Remaining limitations
- No proof-assistant formalization.
- No independent hardware experiment.
- No performance benchmark against path-sum, tensor-network, stabilizer, ZH, or model-counting tools.
- The randomized regression suite is fixed-seed and supportive; universal circuit claims depend on proofs, not sampling.

### Recommendation
**Strong pass on reproducibility for a mathematical/computational note.**

## Referee 5 — exposition and handling editor

### Main attack
The draft was too broad for its actual contribution. Long “human benefit” discussion, speculative future directions, and four complete 16x16 tables made the paper look like a research-program manifesto rather than a focused paper. The correction ledger is useful, but only if the main text already tells the reader what is and is not being claimed.

### Revision accepted
- New title and abstract emphasize signed/integral lifts and boundary results.
- A claim-status table appears near the beginning.
- Broad societal-benefit discussion was removed.
- The next-step section was narrowed to one falsifiable compiler/rewrite benchmark.
- Four full 16x16 tables were removed from the submission body and retained as supplementary data/source.
- Submission manuscript reduced to 30 pages with a much tighter conclusion.
- Internal process language was reduced.

### Residual editorial concern
Thirty pages is still long for some “technical note” venues. A venue with a strict short-note format may require another compression pass, particularly of the correction ledger and literature survey. This is a venue-format issue, not a current correctness blocker.

### Recommendation
**Ready for venue-targeted submission after ordinary house-style formatting.**

# Hostile Referee A — novelty / priority attack

### Adversarial position
“Most of the algebra is standard; the exact quantum circuit material is standard; matrix logic and vector logic predate this work; Boolean path sums and graphical calculi already connect classical Boolean structure to quantum amplitudes. Why should this be published?”

### What survives the attack
The revised paper no longer needs the generic ingredients to be novel. Its defensible package is:

1. an explicit correction-oriented CM-to-phase translation with scalar changes exposed rather than hidden;
2. the typed relation between predicate CMs, reversible oracles, and phase oracles including the exact overlap/carry correction;
3. a fully specified finite CM operator audit, including the shell classification and a reproducible no-fringe result for the tested model;
4. a documented set of failed constructions and model-boundary corrections that prevent several tempting but invalid identifications.

### Residual vulnerability
The exact priority of the CM-specific shell classification and this particular correction package is not established exhaustively. This keeps the novelty score moderate. A novelty-first quantum-computing venue could still desk-reject the paper even if it is correct.

### Hostile recommendation
**Reject as a “new quantum formalism” paper; consider as a technical/correction note if the venue values careful boundary analysis and reproducibility.** The revised manuscript follows the latter framing.

# Hostile Referee B — correctness / model attack

### Adversarial tests attempted
1. Force the paper to identify XOR with signed addition. It fails unless the carry term is retained; the manuscript now states this explicitly.
2. Treat Q as an injective embedding of truth tables. It is not; several distinct CMs map to zero. The manuscript explicitly displays this loss.
3. Treat rotation invariance as a derivation of the Born rule. The support and phase-norm uniqueness results require stated assumptions; the manuscript says so.
4. Treat the 512-shell search as a universal finite-field no-go theorem. The manuscript restricts it to a specific two-branch F2[C4]-linear model, shell, and measurement.
5. Break QP=JQ, the carry formula, or the phase profiles with an independent implementation. No counterexample was found in the tested universes.
6. Attack the exact-circuit section by requiring physical novelty. The manuscript now calls it a classical exact encoding of standard quantum amplitudes.

### Hostile recommendation
**No fatal mathematical counterexample found.** Remaining attack surface is primarily significance/priority rather than correctness.

## Panel synthesis

The reasonable criticisms were incorporated. The criticisms that were not incorporated were those that would demand a different paper (for example, proving a practical speedup that has not been benchmarked) rather than repairing a defect in this one.

The manuscript is now suitable for public preprint release and for targeted journal submission **as a technical/correction-oriented mathematical-computational companion**. It should not be submitted under claims of a new quantum theory, new exact-synthesis method, or demonstrated complexity advantage.
