# Final theorem-level priority search - 27 September 2026

## Target

The search target was not the general Bernstein--Vazirani algorithm or the general polynomial method. It was the precise theorem now stated as Theorem 3.2: exact recovery of every BV secret over a characteristic-two amplitude field under a finite adaptive modal computation with complete recorded linear instruments, arbitrary finite ancillas, and a lower-bound oracle interface allowing fixed pointwise invertible target actions at each recorded query node.

## Result

No source located in the targeted search through 27 September 2026 states the full combination of assumptions and conclusion of Theorem 3.2. The closest sources separate by model: standard complex-amplitude query theory; finite-field oracle/register algebra with complex amplitudes; or finite-field amplitudes in settings not giving this characteristic-two adaptive exact lower bound.

This supports the cautious classification "differentiated specialization after targeted search." It does not establish historical firstness and should not be converted into a "first" or "novel" priority claim without stronger evidence.
