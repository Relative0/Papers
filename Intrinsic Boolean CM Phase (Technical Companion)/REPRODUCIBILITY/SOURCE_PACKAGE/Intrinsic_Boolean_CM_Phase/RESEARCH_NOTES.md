# Source boundaries and research notes

Review date: 17 September 2026.

## What is source-derived

Brian Droncheff's supplied 29-page *Correspondence Matrices; Algorithms for
Propositional Logic* is the source for original CM truth ordering, the four
one-feature arrays, pointwise Boolean composition, geometric transforms, and
support quotient A\B = A AND NOT B. The relevant source portions are sections
2.2-3.3 and the LM material. This paper verifies the identities it needs, not
every claim or derivation in the original manuscript.

The previously supplied *Correspondence Matrices, Cyclic Phase, and Exact
Quantum Encodings* is an earlier AI-assisted working draft, not independent
published validation. This sequel retains its explicit signed-lift boundary
and focuses on the subsequently explored intrinsic Boolean branch.

## What was derived and rerun here

All ring products, scalar classifications, the finite operator inventory,
ANF-related algorithm outputs, synthetic sparse-operation counts, and the
nilpotent regressions are generated by the retained code. The mathematical
proofs identify which finite observations extend to arbitrary circuit length
or numbers of variables. A second script independently rechecks binary
orthogonality and aggregates the retained operator rows.

The subset-interval kernel and shared-tag interaction bound are deductions
worked out in this draft. No claim of historical priority is made. The core
ring and transform are known algebraic objects, and the literature review is
not an exhaustive proof of novelty.

## Primary-literature comparison

Bibliography keys identify the full primary references and links:

- `mizraji`, `mizrajiroots`: vector/matrix logic and square roots of NOT. Related
  motivation but not the same characteristic-two convolution algebra.
- `modal`: finite-field modal quantum theory. Possibility readout differs from
  complete CM-pattern access, and a field differs from this ring with nilpotents.
- `ellerman`: set-based symmetric difference, counting, and lifting comparison.
- `barbier`: Boolean truth-table/ANF Mobius conversion and variablewise methods.
  The preprint is cited by its 2020 arXiv identifier. Its journal-reference
  metadata has a year ambiguity, so no unsupported journal-year resolution is
  used as an important claim.
- `arikan`: the same binary tensor shear kernel in polar-code construction.
  Shared kernel does not prove improved communication or decoding performance.
- `polybori`, `polyboridoc`: established compressed Boolean polynomial methods.
  PolyBoRi was not run or benchmarked in this package.
- `xag`: XOR-AND-inverter graphs for quantum compilation (published 2022).
- `factorized`: Shah/Fiszer/Perkowski, arXiv 2608.27430v2, 1 September 2026.
  Recent factorized-Boolean synthesis preprint; not independently replicated.
- `sparse2026`: Erginbas et al., arXiv 2602.06246, real-valued sparse Mobius
  learning on a Boolean domain; its additive query guarantees are not claimed
  for XOR-valued ANF.
- `nist`: application context for AND/XOR circuit size, depth, privacy-preserving
  protocols, and resource-constrained implementations.
- `dawson`, `amy`, `zh`, `giles`: established Boolean path-sum, verification,
  graphical-calculus, and exact quantum-synthesis comparisons. Their quantum
  amplitude arithmetic is not silently substituted for intrinsic Boolean state
  arithmetic.
- `ad`: a comparison to independent dual/truncated-polynomial tags, not an
  assertion that real differentiation works unchanged in characteristic two.

Recent preprints are identified as such. The research agenda requires fresh
matched experiments instead of inheriting reported performance from the sources.

## Key corrections preserved

1. A four-phase multiplicative group is not complex addition.
2. The self-product is isotropic/non-positive, but the standard free-module
   pairing is not degenerate in the algebraic sense.
3. Equal counts of shell-preserving and intrinsic-unitary matrices do not make
   the sets equal; their freshly measured intersection has 256 elements.
4. A phase-to-ANF pattern is not one sampled quantum outcome. Global phase is
   distinguishable under literal pattern readout.
5. A single abstract oracle layer has no automatically granted scalar-query
   advantage.
6. The marked decoder must remove the known empty-coordinate background.
7. ANF multiplication uses square-free variable-union, not phase convolution.
8. A compact factored circuit is not a free canonical-ANF or constancy oracle.
9. The synthetic fold is equally available to a generic optimizer that knows
   the same identity. It does not demonstrate CM performance superiority.
10. The nilpotent interaction obstruction covers fixed ring-linear circuits,
    not pointwise nonlinear Boolean masking or independent phase registers.
11. The signed lift is a span from integer phase counts, not an embedding of
    the characteristic-two ring into the complex numbers.

## Uses worth testing

The paper prioritizes exact translation certificates, compressed ANF
compilation, nonlinear-gate reduction in matched circuits, bounded change and
interaction analysis, and education. Their practical benefit remains a
hypothesis. No quantum device, general speedup, improved error-correcting code,
clinical benefit, or security guarantee is established by these experiments.
