# Thesis Source Map
## Relevant material in *On the Ultrametric Unconscious, Quantum Consciousness, and Mental Spaces* (2015)

This map records the thesis material that materially bears on unfolding, Mental Space, concepts, decomposition/gluing, cycles/boundaries, logical topology, presheaves, exact sequences, homotopy and homology. Page references are to the PDF page number, with the thesis's printed page usually one page lower in the main body.

The map is descriptive first; the audit judgment is listed separately so that the historical proposal is not silently repaired.

# Standard mathematics versus thesis-specific constructions

## Standard or recognizable mathematics

- topological spaces, subspace topology, covers;
- ultrametric inequality and \(p\)-adic balls;
- abstract simplicial complexes and simplicial maps;
- chain complexes, boundary operators and homology;
- categories and functors;
- presheaf restriction axioms;
- exact sequences and homotopy as intended subject areas (though later thesis definitions are not correct);
- Boolean logic, truth tables and logical equivalence.

## Thesis-specific definitions or constructions

- Mental Space as a topological representation whose points are concepts;
- concepts as special complete-1-skeleton connected simplicial complexes;
- category `Con` of concepts by inclusion;
- distinguishing/unfolding operator with omitted vertex negated;
- interpreting XOR output as conceptual differentiation;
- logical topology/open-sentence rules;
- logical exact sequences of correspondence matrices;
- logical triangles/squares assembled from operators;
- proposed cycle/symmetry and nonzero-boundary/asymmetry correspondence.

## Psychological interpretations rather than theorems

- conscious = asymmetric/distinguishing;
- unconscious = symmetric/indistinguishable;
- unfolding = transition from unconscious to conscious;
- measurement/collapse as a mechanism of concept differentiation;
- words as asymmetrical tools that unfold meaning;
- pink-flower access order as a model of conscious assembly.

## Mathematically defective claims

- ultrametric topology collapsing to indiscrete topology;
- treating overlapping ultrametric balls as always equal without equal-radius qualification;
- original unfolding as though it were the standard boundary operator;
- under-defined repeated unfolding;
- Example 2.7 Boolean gluing identity;
- cycles as symmetry / nonzero boundaries as asymmetry;
- "homotopic" = commutative resolutions;
- logical exactness/quotients without appropriate algebraic category;
- claims suggesting all triangulations are isomorphic.

## Salvageable constructions

- attribute refinement as successive differentiation;
- one-step unfolding as exact-one-false / gated Boolean derivative;
- repeated unfolding as Boolean-lattice rank traversal;
- observational indistinguishability as fibers/equivalence classes;
- concept complexes built from compatibility relations;
- gluing as colimit/descent rather than Boolean conjunction;
- induced homology maps between stages;
- relative/mapping-cone homology as a defect signature;
- symmetry represented by automorphism groups/actions rather than homology.
