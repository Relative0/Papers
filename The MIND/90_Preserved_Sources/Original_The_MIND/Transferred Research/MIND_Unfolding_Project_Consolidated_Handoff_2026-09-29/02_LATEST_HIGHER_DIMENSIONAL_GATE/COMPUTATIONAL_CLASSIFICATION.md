## 1. Exact integral matching verification

The matching verifier represents the changing-carrier problem directly as the pair

\[
(\Delta(Q\times2),\Delta Q^\psi)
\]

and computes integral homology using Smith normal form.  For each boundary `d_n`, a full Smith decomposition is used to obtain a primitive integer basis of `ker d_n`; the image of `d_{n+1}` is then expressed in that kernel basis and Smith-reduced again.  Thus the comparison records both free rank and torsion invariants.

Results for all naturally labelled finite coarse posets with at most four states and all three-valued availability profiles:

| Quantity | Count |
|---|---:|
| total poset/profile cases | 3,450 |
| matching cases checked integrally by SNF | 3,242 |
| matching cases with at least one exclusive inversion | 566 |
| failures of exact integral link formula | **0** |
| profiles with empty exclusive graph and nonzero F2 cone homology | **0** |

## 2. Product-edge-link suspension

For every comparable pair in every naturally labelled coarse poset through five states, the verifier compared

\[
\widetilde H_*(lk_{Delta(Qx2)}((p,0)<(z,1));F_2)
\]

with the one-degree shift of

\[
\widetilde H_*(lk_{Delta Q}[p,z];F_2).
\]

Checks: **1,707**.  Failures: **0**.

## 3. Five-state targeted search

The verifier ran 3,000 deterministic random poset/profile trials over the complete family of naturally labelled five-state transitive closures.  Among them, 639 cases had a nonempty exclusive matching and were checked directly over `F2` against the link formula.

Failures: **0**.

## 4. Boolean high-dimensional examples

## 5. Minimal nonmatching counterexample

For the coarse chain `0<1<2` and profile `(1-only,0-only,0-only)`:

- exclusive graph: two-edge star;
- direct integral relative homology: `H_1 = Z`;
- false independent-edge formula: zero.

The verifier asserts this discrepancy, so a future accidental weakening from `matching` to `forest` will fail the regression suite.
