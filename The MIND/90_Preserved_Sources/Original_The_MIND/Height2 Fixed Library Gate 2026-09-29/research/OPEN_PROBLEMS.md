# Open problems after this gate

## Highest priority: genuine height-two feasibility

Classify RF-IDENT for explicit post-T0 height-at-most-two posets and a fixed supplied query table. It is in NP, has an exact support DP, and is polynomial at height at most one. The three-chain library {010,011} defeats arbitrary-safe-first greedy feasibility at height two. Either a separate NP-hardness reduction or a complete polynomial algorithm would settle a real remaining question.

A tightly specified natural height-two class with a complete feasibility algorithm would also be valuable. Do not treat an optimization hardness result or an exponential DP as a feasibility classification.

## Priority review of exact statements

Before a publication claim, audit the precise restriction/anchor-loss theorem and exact parity-bipartite recurrence against primary work on hereditary and nonhereditary test admissibility. The present search establishes broad overlap, not exhaustive absence of equivalents.

## Lower priority, conditional on the main gate

* Characterize deactivation-free reachable libraries beyond globally isotone tests; universal induced-support safety is already solved.
* Investigate complexity or algorithms exploiting bounded width/treewidth only after specifying which graph (Hasse, comparability or repair) is bounded.
* Distinguish adaptive query choice from a fixed schedule that conditions on each outcome. Existing examples separate full-carrier safety benchmarks, not every possible scheduled-conditioning model.
* Weighted costs, succinct formula representations and logical-fragment-specific algorithms need separate precise inputs and proofs.

The maximal finite gap and complement-closed unbounded-gap questions are resolved here; do not reopen them as conjectures. The exact parity formula is proved, not merely suggested by n=4,8,16.

No return to consciousness interpretations, broad category theory, physical quantum claims or higher-dimensional generalization is needed for these questions.
