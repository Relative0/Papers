# Research Ledger

| Question | Status | Evidence | Counterexample | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primitive concept object | Resolved for modern baseline | Use atomic labeled simplex + complex of atomic concepts + optional semantic layer. | A bare simplex makes every vertex partition a join factorization. | Formalize the structured category and morphisms. |
| Common-face gluing | Standard | Pushout of subcomplex inclusions is the simplicial union after identifying the shared subcomplex. | Two edges glued at a vertex remain a path; no missing cross-edge is created. | Keep pushout as default non-completing gluing. |
| When does gluing yield a simplex? | Resolved | Plain union of two simplices is a simplex only when one is contained in the other. | Two non-nested simplices have vertices a,b from opposite sides but lack edge {a,b}. | If a simplex is desired, declare a completion/filler operation. |
| Tetrahedron from faces | Resolved/corrected | Four triangular faces union to ∂Δ3; adding the 3-simplex gives Δ3. | H2(∂Δ3;F2)=F2 but H2(Δ3;F2)=0. | Choose explicit filler semantics (top-cell addition, flag completion, horn filling, etc.). |
| Recover inputs from pushout | Generally no without provenance | Colimit records the global object, not a unique cover/decomposition. | Filled triangle has three distinct binary coarse join decompositions. | Retain distinguished inclusions/cover or impose canonical factorization criterion. |
| Join independence | Standard and canonical at finest level | Minimal-nonface hypergraph components give finest join factors; 104237 bipartition checks through 5 vertices found zero criterion failures. | Every simplex has empty minimal-nonface hypergraph and therefore decomposes maximally into vertex factors. | Use join only as structural independence, not semantic independence. |
| Logical independence across fixed variable cut | Resolved in supplied CM manuscript | F2 truth-table flattening rank ≤1 iff conjunctive factorization; rank equals minimum XOR sum of conjunctive factors. | Rank depends on the chosen variable bipartition. | Audit priority against classical Boolean decomposition and tensor-rank literature before novelty claims. |
| Relational support independence | Standard | R factors across A\|B iff R=π_A(R)×π_B(R); finite cardinality equality gives an immediate test. | Equality/XOR relations on two bits do not factor. | Consider prime Cartesian factorization of finite relations as a semantic decomposition layer. |
| Sheaf gluing | Standard | For a sheaf, local sections agreeing on pairwise overlaps glue uniquely. | A presheaf on a disconnected two-point space can have compatible local data 0 and 1 with no global section. | Use sheaf condition as consistency criterion; use higher descent only for richer targets. |
| Cosheaf role | Promising standard analogue | Cosheaves aggregate local data covariantly by colimits. | Cosheaf aggregation does not by itself imply recoverable semantic factorization. | Test sheaf-for-consistency + cosheaf-for-aggregation dual architecture. |
| Mayer-Vietoris as interaction detector | Useful but not sufficient for independence | Connecting maps record global cycles created through overlap; circle from two arcs gives nonzero connecting map. | Contractible join-irreducible complexes can have trivial homology. | Use MV as obstruction/diagnostic, never as a complete independence test. |
| Unfolding inverse to gluing | No in general | Face deletion/boundary and filling are not mutual inverses without retained filler/provenance. | ∂Δ3 does not determine that a 3-cell should be inserted under plain complex semantics. | Study a restricted category of declared fillers/structured covers if an adjunction is desired. |
| Face decomposition vs disentanglement | Resolved as distinct | Faces overlap and encode incidence; independent factors require a product/join/semantic split criterion. | Δ(V) has many face decompositions and trivial join decomposability. | Keep two operators and separate notation. |
| Novel theorem-level contribution | Not established | Core gluing, pushout, sheaf, join, conceptual blending, FCA and Boolean decomposition all have close antecedents. | 2021 conceptual blending already models concepts by category-theoretic pushout/amalgam; 2025/26 work explicitly studies finest join support systems. | Attack structured multi-layer prime decomposition as the next theorem problem. |
| Project end-state | Outcome 4: requires additional structure | Bare simplices/complexes make composition standard and independence often trivial; labels/logic/sheaf/CM repair this. | Simplex-only model cannot distinguish substantive independence from arbitrary vertex partition. | Define and prove uniqueness/algorithmics for the structured decomposition invariant. |
