|---|---|---|---|
| Can arbitrary structured d-DNNF expose the full prime partition directly in polynomial time? | Not established | noncanonical OR structure; 2024 nonclosure results obstruct obvious transformations | narrow to compressed SDD / better vtree, keep SAT fallback |

| Is incremental SAT itself novel? | No | DAC 2008 and later bi-decomposition literature | position as comparator/integration |
