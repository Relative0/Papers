# Literature and Priority Notes for the Unified Calculus

These notes separate standard antecedents from project-specific synthesis targets.

## Standard / close antecedents

1. **Finite topology and preorder semantics.** Finite topological spaces are equivalent to finite preordered sets; the space is T0 exactly when the specialization preorder is antisymmetric. This makes the carrier-level preorder formulation standard finite-space mathematics.

2. **Rough-set information systems.** A set of attributes induces an indiscernibility equivalence relation by equality of attribute values. Adding attributes refines indiscernibility. Dynamic/incremental attribute-reduction literature studies changing attribute sets and object sets.

3. **Quotient saturation.** For a surjection/quotient map q, a subset A is saturated iff A=q^{-1}(q(A)), equivalently iff it is a union of fibers. The conditioning-prism cartesianity criterion is therefore a finite observation-system specialization of standard saturation theory.

4. **Lexicographic sums.** Replacing each element p of a poset P by a fiber poset B_p and ordering all of B_p below all of B_q whenever p<q is the standard lexicographic sum/substitution construction.

5. **Mapping-cone octahedron.** For composable maps f and g, the cones of f, g, and gf fit into the standard octahedral distinguished triangle. Using that triangle to split total refinement defect into expansion and thinning pieces is an application, not a new homological-algebra theorem.

6. **Dynamic epistemic logic.** Public announcement restricts a model to worlds satisfying the announced formula. More general action models/product update can copy worlds by pairing worlds with possible events. This is close in spirit to model-changing informational dynamics, though it does not supply the ProLT specialization-order/homology package.

7. **Formal Concept Analysis / attribute exploration.** Attribute exploration is an established interactive query-based method for acquiring structured knowledge. Adding attributes and incrementally updating concept lattices also has a literature.

8. **Test Cover and adaptive combinatorial search.** Minimum separating test families are classical Test Cover / Test Collection problems and are NP-hard. Adaptive test strategies are also well studied, including round complexity and deterministic/randomized variants.

## Project-specific synthesis candidates that remain worth auditing

A. Canonical ProLT refinement factorization through the lexicographic fiber expansion F.

B. Localization of support/refinement non-cartesianity to the fiber-expansion face of the conditioning prism.

C. Fine-measurable outcome theorem: newly enabled measurement iff the expansion-conditioning square is non-cartesian.

D. A ProLT-specific mixed total-cofiber invariant for refinement/conditioning squares.

E. Generalized relative observation dimension for arbitrary preorder refinement, with the current post-T0 observation dimension and pre-T0 Test Cover as special regimes.

F. Adaptive observation design with explicit ProLT topological/homological constraints and branch-local T0 frontiers.

The likely novelty, if any, is in the integration and the constrained ProLT-specific theorems, not in the individual standard ingredients.
