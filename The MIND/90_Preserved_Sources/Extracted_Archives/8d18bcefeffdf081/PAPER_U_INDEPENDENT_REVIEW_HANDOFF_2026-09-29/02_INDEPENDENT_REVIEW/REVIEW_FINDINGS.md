## Strongest hostile objections

**'The mathematics is standard.'** This objection succeeds against a research-novelty claim. Partition topology, metric identification, threshold quotients, profinite reconstruction and full-tree stabilizer machinery have direct predecessors. The exact adaptive normality criterion was not located as a published named theorem, but its short derivation from standard ingredients gives no warrant for a first-priority claim. The paper's value must be expository and corrective.

**'The correction is directed only at the author's own thesis.'** There is an explicit error in that thesis, independently rechecked in the supplied PDF: it connects ultrametricity to indiscrete topology and to topological indistinguishability. The note also clarifies the mathematical status of cluster/perceptual arguments in the cited literature. The historical revision credits their existing observational analogy; it does not manufacture a stronger error for them to have made. The adaptive nonnormality example and completion boundary make the note more useful than a bare thesis erratum. An editor may nevertheless find its audience too narrow; that is a contribution-fit risk, not a mathematical defect.

**'The cognitive language is post hoc.'** No theorem chooses meaningful features, their ordering, an observer, or a psychological time scale. The criticism is valid against empirical or full-bi-logic claims; v0.3 makes neither. The note is a conditional mathematical clarification, and should be judged as such.

**'The p-adics are unnecessary.'** Correct for the general theorems. They remain a compact specialization giving canonical residue-ring maps. No new arithmetic theory is advertised. No XOR, martingale expansion, noisy-channel theory, or Paper F machinery has been imported.

**'The group result assumes the symmetry it needs.'** The full regular-tree contract is indeed strong. It is explicit and is exactly what permits independent sections and transitivity on infinite rays. Counterexamples for smaller groups and nonregular geometries are part of the review and manuscript. This is a limitation rather than an invalid theorem.

## What publication readiness does and does not mean

The independent content gate supports submission of this narrowed note, subject to normal venue selection and formatting. It does not predict acceptance, verify a particular journal's present policy, or authorize external submission. A pure topology/group-theory journal demanding substantial original results would have a sound reason to reject it. No theorem expansion is required to finish this request; expanding claims would reopen correctness, priority and historical gates.

The next useful step is to identify a venue that explicitly accepts synthesis/correction notes, then adapt only its required format. Continue in this task while these decisions and evidence are active. A general-purpose capable model such as gpt-6-sol at medium reasoning should be sufficient for that bounded editorial task; this is a suitability recommendation, not a measured price or cache-saving claim. External submission remains a separate action requiring the author's explicit instruction.
