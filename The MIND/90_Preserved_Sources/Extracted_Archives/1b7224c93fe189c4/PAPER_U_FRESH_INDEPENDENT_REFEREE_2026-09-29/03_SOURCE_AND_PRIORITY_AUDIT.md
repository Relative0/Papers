## Primary-source ledger

Direct predecessor to cognitive prefix classes, not just a generic use of p-adic numbers. A regenerated PDF date is not a publication date. Supports TeX line 41.

2. **Khrennikov–Yurova, 2017, §§4 and 9.** [Author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.04681); [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pbiomolbio.2017.02.003). 4. **Mémoli–Smith–Wan, published 2023, §2.1.** [Published PDF](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/article/download/3706/3365); [publisher record](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/article/view/3706). Journal of Computational Geometry 14(1), 78–143; publisher record says 19 September 2023 (other indexing dates should not replace it). Full published PDF inspected, pp.87–88, Equation (5), Remark 9, Definition 10. Exact defining relation: x ~_t x' iff u_X(x,x') ≤ t. Quotient distances between different classes retain u_X; within a class they are zero. Open quotients use a strict inequality. This is a direct predecessor for Paper U's closed-threshold construction, properly credited at line 130. The author's general finite, unequal-ball partition is not verbatim this single-threshold definition.

Those earlier propositions were identified through Ishiki's references, not independently audited here; 2023 is not established as the earliest date of this construction.

## Exact adaptive criterion: search outcome and limits
Queries include frontier/cutset/antichain/partition pointwise stabilizers, normalizers, full rooted-tree automorphism groups, maximal prefix codes, and adaptive normality. The exact queries are in 03_SEARCH_QUERIES.json. Standard finite-level kernels and section decompositions are direct antecedents. I found no independently inspected source stating precisely this relative, unequal-per-old-cell formulation. Its short derivation from standard theory makes an original-research novelty claim especially premature. Unknown earlier statements, unindexed books, inaccessible works and equivalent terminology remain possible. No firstness claim is certified.
