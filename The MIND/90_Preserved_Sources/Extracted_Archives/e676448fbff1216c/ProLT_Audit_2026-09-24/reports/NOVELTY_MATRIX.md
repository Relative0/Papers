## What remains after removing ProLT notation?

The focused theorem is about an arbitrary finite poset P and a map b:P->{0,1}: retain x<=y only when b(x)<=b(y). Logical formulas are not used in the topological proof. This general form is both stronger and clearer. The propositional realization belongs in a brief application section, not in every theorem statement.

Similarly, the dimension problem is about nested orders Q subset P and separating Q-upsets. The matrix with zeros on Q, ones on P\Q, and question marks elsewhere is an exact translation into known generalized Boolean covering. The strongest defensible future contribution would concern the special structure forced by a coarse chain, Boolean lattice, or another constrained family, rather than the existence of a minimum parameter itself. The corrected parameter already gives useful certificates: the final five-state compensating pair has minimum one bit, the thirteen-state torsion pair minimum two, and the eight-state noncollapse pair minimum three. These are exact applications of the coloring theorem, not new general complexity results.

## Strong negative novelty findings

The package should not claim new finite-space homology, new mapping-cone theory, new cohomological integration, new Dowker duality, a new general rooted-forest determinant formula, or a new general persistence construction. The coarse-chain homotopy types have a specific Ferrers-graph antecedent. The 2-dimension equality is false, and correcting it reveals closer generalized-covering prior art.

These findings do not make the project worthless. They concentrate its possible contribution in an exact binary-labelled-poset preservation theorem, an explicit algorithm/certificate, and sharp boundaries in bit count and height.

## Adjacent areas: equivalence, useful bridge, or speculation?

**Real equivalences:** finite binary information systems and signature preorders; Dowker complexes of the observation incidence relation; closure-operator proofs of repair criteria; generalized Boolean rectangle covering and conflict coloring; rooted incidence matrices and graph cycle spaces.

**Useful mathematical bridges, not automatic equivalences:** formal concept lattices (whose full lattice may have cone endpoints even when a chosen proper-part complex does not); rough-set reducts (fixed available attributes versus arbitrary designed upsets); SAT theta/independence complexes (different complexes and semantics); abstract interpretation (adjoints resemble abstraction/concretization but operational soundness is additional); Kripke semantics (positive persistence along the order, but arbitrary classical negation is not intuitionistic negation); database dependence and constraint consistency (requires explicit relational/sheaf data); dynamic sparsification (same-vertex order thinning is a constrained deletion model).

**Speculative and presently unsupported:** neural/mental-state interpretations, cognitive relevance of particular Betti numbers, physical significance, or claims that the arbitrary signature coboundary detects contextuality. None should appear as a demonstrated application. Higher-order interaction spectral sequences should be deferred until the two-coordinate exact sequence demonstrably fails to answer a concrete question.
