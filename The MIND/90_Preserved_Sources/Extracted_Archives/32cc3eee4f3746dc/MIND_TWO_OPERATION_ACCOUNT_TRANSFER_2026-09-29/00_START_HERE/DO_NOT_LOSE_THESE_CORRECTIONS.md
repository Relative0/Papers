# Do not lose these corrections

These are the most important corrections/frozen lessons from the current research line.

1. **Refinement and measurement are distinct axes.** Refinement changes available distinctions/observation structure; conditioning changes the surviving possibility support.

2. **The classical base is not quantum measurement.** Canonical Boolean/CM measurement is restriction/conditioning via commuting Boolean effects. Nonclassical claims require additional structure.

3. **World-level refinement is preorder thinning.** The apparent pre-T0 split vs post-T0 thinning distinction is largely quotient-level.

4. **U2 correction:** after quotienting, splitting one old quotient point can reveal a new strict order *inside that split fiber*. What refinement cannot do is create comparability between descendants of two distinct coarse-incomparable points.

5. **U3 survives:** finite refinement factors through the lexicographic sum of new-coordinate fiber profiles. The construction itself is standard lexicographic-poset mathematics; the useful part is the canonical ProLT factorization.

6. **U4 correction:** the mapping-cone result is an octahedral/distinguished-triangle long exact sequence, not a direct-sum decomposition in general.

7. **U5 survives:** the expansion/support square is cartesian iff every surviving coarse point retains its full refined label profile.

8. **U6 hypothesis is essential:** coarse measurability iff cartesianity/fiber-defect-zero only under the explicit assumption that the event is fine-measurable.

9. **Unrestricted observation design is largely standard territory.** Arbitrary target preorder realization by Boolean tests should not be sold as novel.

10. **Strong negative adaptive result:** with an unrestricted Boolean query pool, homotopy-neutrality alone imposes no worst-case depth penalty. Balanced isotone/redundant questions can achieve information-theoretic depth.

11. **Theorem A1:** on an n-chain under the exact ProLT one-bit homotopy-neutral query language, safe adaptive identification has depth ceil(log2 n); every safe tree needs a redundant query on at least one root-to-leaf path; exactly one is sufficient on a worst path; forbidding all redundant queries makes complete identification impossible.

12. **A1 novelty discipline:** treat it as a plausible narrow ProLT-specific corollary, not a new general decision-tree theorem.

13. **Next step:** move to post-T0 height <= 2 with a fixed observation library and the exact repair-forest / relative-boundary admissibility criterion.
