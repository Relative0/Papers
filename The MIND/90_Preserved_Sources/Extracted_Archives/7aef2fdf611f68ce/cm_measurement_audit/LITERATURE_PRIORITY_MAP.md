# Literature and Priority Map — CM Measurement Audit

**Search horizon:** material located through 28 September 2026.  
**Standard:** construction/theorem-level comparison where sources could be located. This is a bounded priority audit, not proof of global historical firstness.

## 1. Main priority conclusion

The historical and modern CM program should **not** claim firstness for matrix representations of propositional logic, vector/matrix logical operators, Dirac-style notation for logic, or the general use of projection operators to encode logical truth. Those areas have substantial antecedents.

The strongest defensible CM-side contribution remains the one already adopted by the current CM/LM foundations manuscript: a **typed formula-valued LM/CM architecture** linking polarity lifts, valuation, labelled frame transformations, logical pairing, and aligned Boolean operator superposition. The measurement theory developed in this audit is best positioned as a **rigorous integration and correction**: CM truth data are mapped to standard finite event projectors, while ProLT supplies a separate theory of observational refinement.

For the 2015 consciousness motivation, the closest pre-thesis antecedent found is particularly important: **Andrei Khrennikov's 2001 preprint / 2003 paper explicitly combines quantum-like cognitive measurement, consciousness as a self-measuring system, and p-adic hierarchical mental space.** That connection should be credited prominently in any historical account.

## 3. Source details and stable links

### Matrix and vector logic

**August Stern (1988), _Matrix Logic: Theory and Applications_.**  
Elsevier / ScienceDirect: https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9780444704320/matrix-logic  
The publisher describes a formulation of logic using matrices and vector spaces and explicitly discusses a connection with Dirac quantum formalism.

**Eduardo Mizraji (1992), "Vector logics: The matrix-vector representation of logical calculus," _Fuzzy Sets and Systems_ 50(2), 179-185.**  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0165-0114(92)90216-Q  
The abstract states that binary operations such as conjunction, disjunction, implication, and Sheffer's connective are represented by matrices acting on Kronecker products of vectors.

**Eduardo Mizraji (1996), "The Operators of Vector Logic," _Mathematical Logic Quarterly_ 42(1), 27-40.**  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/malq.19960420104

**William Bricken (internally dated March 1997), "Notes on Matrix Techniques for Logic."**  
https://wbricken.com/pdfs/01bm/01math/03math-supporting/math-tangential/04matrix-tech.pdf  
The current website establishes access to the note and the document itself carries the March 1997 date. It does **not** by itself establish when the note first became publicly available or peer-reviewed; that chronology should remain qualified.

## 4. What can still plausibly be claimed as contribution

The evidence supports a contribution framed around **integration and precise separation of roles**, not first invention of projective logic.

A defensible current contribution would be:

- a canonical typed map from compact CM truth data to valuation-space sharp effects;
- a complete finite classification showing how compact-CM idempotence depends on the multiplication law;
- a proof that the natural diagonal measurement algebra is classical/commutative;
- an exact separation between observation refinement and selective outcome update, linked to the current ProLT formalism;
- a theorem showing the thesis's context-relative "purity" is cylinder extension;
- explicit counterexamples showing symmetry-stabilizer decrease is not equivalent to increased distinguishability;
- a clean boundary specifying what additional axioms are needed before modal/contextual/quantum measurement claims are meaningful.

This combination appears substantially more precise than the 2015 formulation even where its individual ingredients are classical or have prior art.

## 5. Priority cautions for a future manuscript

Do not claim:

- invention of matrix logic;
- first vector/matrix representation of Boolean connectives;
- first bra-ket representation of logic;
- first logical projectors or truth-eigenvalue observables;
- first quantum-like model of cognitive measurement;
- first p-adic mental-space model;
- contextuality from ambient-variable dependence alone.

A future paper should instead say exactly which theorem or typed bridge is proved and compare it directly with the constructions above.
