# Response to the independent AI referee — v0.5

The referee recommended revision of the 29 September 2026 v0.4 draft before submission. The present v0.5 addresses the bounded statement, proof, and positioning issues identified in that report. This response records changes for review; it does not convert the AI report into human peer review or establish publication priority.

| Referee finding | v0.5 response | Verification / limit |
|---|---|---|

The text explicitly states that for `m=1`, unfolding is Boolean negation. | Direct Boolean evaluation; v0.5 source assertions and targeted check. No later structural theorem depends on the former witness. |
