## Required theorem-level sources
- **Khrennikov 2007, §4:** [author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-bio/0610013), BioSystems 90(3), 656–675, DOI 10.1016/j.biosystems.2007.02.004. Initial preprint 5 October 2006; consulted v3 dated 1 February 2007. Full text §4 pp.9–10 read. Short passage: “equivalence classes of mental states are interpreted as associations”. Fixed initial k digits and their balls directly precede cognitive prefix classes. A regenerated PDF date is not its publication date.
- **Khrennikov–Yurova 2017, §§4 and 9:** [author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.04681), DOI 10.1016/j.pbiomolbio.2017.02.003, Progress in Biophysics and Molecular Biology 130, 2–14. Online 15 February 2017, issue November; arXiv 15 April 2017. Full passages pp.7–9 and 18–20 inspected. “non-distinguishable with respect to observations”: output equality for every input gives observational equivalence; later p-adic/ultrametric latent states supply a predecessor architecture. It is not the exact ball-collapse theorem; dynamics needs congruence.
- **Kleiner 2020, §§3.3–3.4:** [v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.03223v2), Entropy 22(6), 609, DOI 10.3390/e22060609, published 30 May 2020. Preprint initial 7 July 2019, v2 20 April 2020. Full author text pp.16–19 inspected, equations (3.6)–(3.8). “intersubjectively well-defined references to experience”: automorphism-orbit quotients of sequences, not Paper U's particular metric. MDPI access failed and PMC challenged the browser; the author version supports the comparison, not a claim of exact journal collation.
- **Mémoli–Smith–Wan, published 2023 §2.1:** [publisher PDF](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/article/download/3706/3365), [publisher record](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/article/view/3706), DOI 10.20382/jocg.v14i1a4, JoCG 14(1), 78–143. Publisher date 19 September 2023. Published pp.87–88, Equation (5), Remark 9 and Definition 10 inspected. The exact relation is x~_t x' iff u_X(x,x')≤t; distinct classes retain u_X and coincident classes have zero distance. Direct predecessor to the closed-threshold construction. Open quotient uses strict inequality. Do not substitute preprint Definition 9 for published Definition 10.
- **Ishiki Proposition 2.31:** [v3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.00305v3), initial 1 February 2023, v3 16 February 2023. Definition 2.2 and Proposition 2.31, pp.11–12, inspected in full text. The formula is e_i within B_i and max{e_i(x,p_i),r(i,j),e_j(p_j,y)} between pieces. Set e_i=0 and r=δ_P to obtain the collapse. The “pleasant” extra condition concerns genuine internal metrics and is unnecessary. Ishiki points further back to arXiv:2104.12450 Proposition 3.1 and Topology and its Applications 327 (2023), 108442, Proposition 2.1; these were leads, not independently audited here. The recovered ledger uses the corrected Definition 2.2 locator; an earlier draft of this task's ledger mistyped it as 2.30.
- **Partition topology:** [Iyer–Shrivastava published PDF](https://ijpam.eu/contents/2012-74-3/3/3.pdf), 74(3), 313–320 (2012), Definition 1 p.313. Full text read. “these subsets form the basis for the topology”. Definition reference, not a first-priority claim.
- **Metric identification:** [Pirttimäki PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01157), 2019, Theorem 5.3, pp.18–19. Full text inspected. “determines the same topology as the quotient map”. Zero-distance quotient topology is established; this does not authorize switching Paper U's source topologies.
- **Profinite topology:** [Stacks tag 08ZY](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/08ZY), complete Lemma 5.22.2 and proof read; live access 29 September 2026, no single original publication date displayed, visible corrections in 2018. “a cofiltered limit of finite discrete spaces”. This concerns compact Hausdorff totally disconnected topology; a numerical metric needs separate calibration.
- **Completion:** [Dovgoshey v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.04228v1), 2025, Theorem 10.25 p.83 and Proposition 10.29 p.85 inspected. The former relates labeled-tree isomorphism to isometric completions. The latter equips maximal chains with the meet/diameter metric and identifies that space isometrically with completion. This justified the added locator.
- **Tree groups:** [Branch Groups](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0510294v2), Bartholdi–Grigorchuk–Šunić, Handbook of Algebra 3 (2003), 989–1112; arXiv deposit October 2005. §§1.2.2–1.2.3, pp.17–22, read. “any choice of vertex permutations at and below u is possible”. Portraits, sections, level kernels, products and wreath cardinalities are standard ingredients. [Bartholdi–Sidki](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0308127), initial 2003, consulted version 14 March 2006, §3 was also checked for level/wreath structure. Neither inspected passage provided the exact adaptive iff.
- **Frontiers:** [Lawson–Vdovina](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.13254), first posted 2019, §12 Lemma 12.1 and nearby pp.34–36 inspected. Finite maximal prefix codes and elementary expansions supply the combinatorial setting.
- **Entropy:** [Perotti–Almeira–Saracco](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.02911), Physical Review E 101, 062148 (2020), Equations (7),(10), p.3 of author version read. Conditional hierarchical entropy accounting is prior. No equality between group index and entropy is supplied or implied.

## Primary historical inspection
**Matte Blanco:** [authorized Routledge Google Books preview](https://books.google.com/books?id=GmEPEAAAQBAJ). The [publisher preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780429908361_A35173827/preview-9780429908361_A35173827.pdf) verifies original Duckworth 1975, Karnac 1998 and Routledge 2018 provenance, but ends before the requested later pages. It alone would not verify the claims. Actual browser page images were read, with printed page numbers and publisher permission visible:
- [38–39](https://books.google.com/books?id=GmEPEAAAQBAJ&pg=PA38): generalization and relation/converse symmetry; stronger treatment of members/parts/whole as identical.
- [45–46](https://books.google.com/books?id=GmEPEAAAQBAJ&pg=PA45): negation and opposition discussion; not ordinary Boolean observation algebra.
- [107–108](https://books.google.com/books?id=GmEPEAAAQBAJ&pg=PA107): translation/unfolding and becoming conscious of content; not a law of resolution-index time.
- [146–149](https://books.google.com/books?id=GmEPEAAAQBAJ&pg=PA146): cardinal correspondence and qualifications distinguishing inclusion from equicardinality and external description from modeled treatment. Page 149: “Our interpretation becomes in this way a working hypothesis”.

All ten requested page images were inspected, including upper/lower sections. Strong identity language and external-observer qualification coexist. The note represents both and deliberately supplies only a restricted observational model. Physical-1975-copy collation and all-edition equivalence are not claimed. Source images are not redistributed.

**Lauro-Grotto:** [author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/236697092_The_Unconscious_as_an_Ultrametric_Set), American Imago 64(4), 535–543, Winter 2007 issue / 2008 copyright/publication listing, DOI 10.1353/aim.2008.0009. Evidence class: author-attributed public full-text extraction with printed page boundaries, not fresh page images. Pages 537–539 were read. The perceptual analogy on p.539 is explicit: “we perceive it as though it were a single point”. The text also overstates within-cluster equidistance and indistinguishability from ultrametricity alone. Paper U fairly credits the analogy while correcting that inference; it does not attribute a formal indiscrete-topology theorem to Lauro-Grotto.

The Florence repository [record](https://flore.unifi.it/handle/2158/373430) identifies a deposited postprint, but the PDF endpoint returned 403. Its inconsistent page field was not adopted. Matte-Blanco was checked against his own images, not Lauro-Grotto's quotations.
