# Corrected Ultrametric Model

## 1. State space

Let `A={0,...,m-1}` be a finite alphabet and

`X=A^N`.

A point `x=(x_0,x_1,...)` is a fully specified hierarchical state. Let

`L(x,y)=min{j>=0:x_j != y_j}`

for `x!=y`, i.e. the common-prefix length, and `L(x,x)=infinity`.

Fix `0<lambda<1` and define

`d_lambda(x,y)=0` if `x=y`, otherwise `lambda^{L(x,y)}`.

### Proposition 1

`d_lambda` is an ultrametric.

**Proof.** If `x` and `y` agree through depth `a`, and `y` and `z` agree through depth `b`, then `x` and `z` agree through at least `min(a,b)`. Hence

`L(x,z) >= min(L(x,y),L(y,z))`,

so

`d(x,z) <= max(d(x,y),d(y,z))`.

---

## 2. Concepts as balls/cylinders

For a finite word `s=(s_0,...,s_{k-1})`, define

`B_s={x in X : x_0=s_0,...,x_{k-1}=s_{k-1}}`.

These are the depth-`k` concepts.

### Proposition 2

Each `B_s` is a clopen ultrametric ball, and the family of all such sets is laminar: any two are disjoint or one contains the other.

### Proposition 3

`B_t subseteq B_s` iff `s` is a prefix of `t`.

Thus adding a coordinate means refining a concept.

---

## 3. Observation quotients

Define

`pi_k:X->A^k`, `pi_k(x)=(x_0,...,x_{k-1})`.

Define

`x ~_k y  <=>  pi_k(x)=pi_k(y)`.

### Theorem 4 (finite-resolution quotient)

For every `k`:

1. `~_k` is an equivalence relation;
2. its equivalence classes are exactly the depth-`k` concept balls `B_s`;
3. `X_k=X/~_k` is naturally isomorphic to `A^k`;
4. if the hierarchy is full regular `m`-ary, `|X_k|=m^k`;
5. `~_{k+1}` refines `~_k`.

This is the mathematically correct source of resolution-limited indistinguishability.

---

## 4. Filtration of observable information

Let

`F_k = sigma(pi_k)`.

Then

`F_0 subseteq F_1 subseteq F_2 subseteq ...`.

For the product topology on `A^N`, the sigma-algebra generated by the union of all `F_k` is the Borel sigma-algebra.

An observable `f:X->Y` is available at resolution `k` iff it factors through `pi_k`:

`f = f_bar o pi_k`.

Equivalently, `f` is constant on each `~_k` class.

This gives a precise version of “the system does not distinguish members of a class.”

---

## 5. Symmetry as invariance

Let `G_k` be a group of hierarchy-preserving permutations acting inside the fibers of `pi_k`.

A level-`k` observable is symmetric if

`f(gx)=f(x)` for every `g in G_k`.

When `G_k` acts transitively on each fiber, every `G_k`-invariant observable is constant on that fiber. Thus symmetry can *explain* observational equivalence without identifying points literally.

## 6. p-adic specialization

Take `m=p` prime and `lambda=p^{-1}`. Map

`x=(x_0,x_1,...)`

to

`Phi(x)=sum_{j>=0} x_j p^j in Z_p`.

Then for `x!=y`,

`v_p(Phi(x)-Phi(y))=L(x,y)`

and therefore

`|Phi(x)-Phi(y)|_p = p^{-L(x,y)} = d(x,y)`.

So the prefix space and `Z_p` are isometric under the standard digit identification.

Depth-`k` concepts become residue classes

`a + p^k Z_p`,

i.e. congruence classes modulo `p^k`.

### Important distinction for composite `m`

For arbitrary branching number `m`, the safest fundamental object is the `m`-ary rooted tree/prefix space. Prime-base p-adic field structure should not be silently generalized to composite `m`. Composite-base `m`-adic rings/trees can still be used, but their algebra differs from a field.

## 7. Concept operations

For finite prefixes `s,t`:

- **refinement:** append symbols;
- **coarsening:** delete final symbols;
- **intersection:** deeper compatible prefix, else empty;
- **join/LCA:** longest common prefix;
- **distance between leaves:** scale determined by LCA depth;
- **distance between concepts:** may be induced from the tree/LCA or Hausdorff-style variants if needed;
- **forgetting an attribute:** a projection, not set intersection.

The thesis's operation that extracts a shared coordinate from two conflicting cylinders should therefore be written as a projection such as

`rho_1(B_{011}) = {x_1=1}` and `rho_1(B_{111})={x_1=1}`,

not as `B_{011} intersection B_{111}`.

## 8. Probability and information

Under the uniform product measure,

`mu(B_s)=m^{-|s|}`.

Conditional on a depth-`k` concept, every child has probability `1/m` in the uniform model.

Information revealed by one regular refinement is

`Delta I = log_2 m` bits.

At depth `k`,

`I_k = k log_2 m` bits.

With nonuniform conditional probabilities `p(a|s)`, use

`mu(B_{sa})=mu(B_s)p(a|s)`

and information increment

`-log_2 p(a|s)`.

This turns “unfolding” into an information-refinement process.

---

## 9. A minimal dynamics

A conservative dynamic model has two components:

1. **latent transition** `T:X->X` or a Markov kernel `K(x,dy)` acting on the underlying hierarchy;
2. **observation schedule** `k(t)` or more general partitions `P_t`, determining the resolution available at time `t`.

The observer sees

`Y_t = pi_{k(t)}(X_t)`.

This sharply separates mental-state dynamics from observation/coarse-graining dynamics.

If p-adic analysis later proves useful, `T` can be taken from p-adic dynamical systems or hierarchical diffusion. But the theory is already coherent without that layer.

---

## 10. Strong formulation

> **Ultrametric-unconscious principle.** A cognitive system may possess a fine-grained hierarchical state space while its current observables factor through a coarse hierarchical quotient. States in the same quotient fiber are therefore behaviorally/observationally equivalent at that resolution despite remaining distinct, Hausdorff-separable states of the underlying ultrametric space.

This is the principal correction to the 2015 model.
