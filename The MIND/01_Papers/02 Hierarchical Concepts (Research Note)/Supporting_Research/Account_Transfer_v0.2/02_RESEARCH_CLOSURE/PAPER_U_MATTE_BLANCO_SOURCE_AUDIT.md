# Paper U: Matte-Blanco primary-source audit

**Date:** 29 September 2026. **Conclusion:** the revised interpretation is a deliberately restricted analogy, not a formalization of full bi-logic.

## 2. The significant correction to v0.1

The abstract of v0.1 attributes to Matte-Blanco a distinction in which class members are treated as equivalent **without being literally identical**. That is not an adequate account of the primary text's stronger formulations on printed p.39. The revision no longer presents this distinction as Matte-Blanco's own limited claim. Instead it says that the mathematical model deliberately retains distinct latent objects while identifying them only relative to an explicitly selected observer.

This is not a reason to discard the mathematical construction. It is a reason to change the historical and interpretive claim. A weaker operational model can be useful, but it must be identified as weaker.

## 4. Why the distinctions are mathematically unavoidable

**Relation symmetry is not observational equivalence.** An observation map supplies a specific equivalence relation. A general binary relation can be symmetric without being transitive or reflexive. Conversely, the fact that one relation, equality of observation codes, is symmetric says nothing about whether an independent parent-of, before, contains, or desires relation is symmetrized. Those relations would have to be included in the model and a rule for their treatment supplied.

**A quotient does not assert literal equality of representatives.** It constructs a new set of classes. A statement in the quotient is not automatically a statement in the original set. Moreover the equality of a point with a set of points would require a change of type; q(x) being a class label does not provide one. This is the precise reason the new manuscript uses restricted observability rather than a claim to model every part/whole assertion.

**The accessible logic remains classical.** The complement of a union of cells is another union of cells. Consequently an accessible proposition and its negation remain distinguished in the Boolean algebra, even when many underlying point distinctions are unavailable. Coarse resolution can hide some propositions; it does not invalidate the complement operation on the propositions that remain.

**Infinity is optional.** Every finite example in the verification suite has the same quotient mechanism. Thus the mechanism cannot, by itself, imply an infinite-set ontology. A regular infinite boundary supplies infinite cardinality by construction, not by psychological deduction.

**Resolution is not time.** The parameter k specifies an observation contract. It need not be a chronological variable. A stochastic or deterministic cognitive transition law would be additional structure and would need independent motivation. The separate Paper F project is not used to fill this gap.

## 5. Lauro-Grotto and the substantive correction target

Lauro-Grotto's *The Unconscious as an Ultrametric Set* is American Imago 64(4), 535-543, DOI 10.1353/aim.2008.0009. Printed pp.537-539 were inspected visually in the author PDF. They connect cluster structure with indistinguishability and symmetrization. The relevant mathematical caution is independent of the psychology: equal distances to an external observer are not equal internal pairwise distances, and neither condition implies zero distance or an indiscrete metric topology.

For example, take d(a,b)=1/2 and d(a,c)=d(b,c)=1. Points a and b are equally distant from c, yet they are different and metrically separable. Even a finite equilateral ultrametric space is discrete, not indiscrete. An observation that records only the larger cluster is needed to make a and b indistinguishable at that observation level. This gives the note a correction target beyond the author's private thesis, without suggesting that all ultrametric cognition literature makes the same error.

Murtagh's 2012 review and 2014 article are relevant historical extensions. Their readings do not authorize replacing the primary text's stronger claims by the narrower operational interpretation adopted here.

## 6. Wording retained in the revision

The manuscript's interpretation section cites the primary page locators rather than citing a broad secondary summary. It states that nested membership is represented, relation-converse symmetry is not automatically represented, latent identity is not asserted, the accessible algebra remains Boolean, and neither infinity nor conscious unfolding follows from the quotient. It makes no claim that Matte-Blanco supplied the modern partition-topology or pseudometric construction.

## 7. Residual checks for the next referee gate

A historian or mathematically informed psychoanalytic reader should collate the short cited passages against an authorized print or image edition, particularly pp.38-39 and 107-108. This is a source-verification task, not a missing mathematical lemma. A stronger fidelity claim would require a separate relational/logical model; the present note deliberately does not make it.
