# Paper U: adversarial attacks and revision decisions

**Method:** one model carried out separate specialist-style and hostile passes with deliberately different objectives. These are not reports from independent human referees or separately instantiated research agents. The proof and source evidence, rather than a claimed panel authority, supports the conclusions. This is the closure gate, not the final publication-readiness scorecard.

## Attack 1: "This is all textbook mathematics"

**Strongest hostile case.** A partition map gives a kernel equivalence relation. Its initial topology is a partition topology; the T0 quotient is discrete. A pulled-back metric gives the same zero classes. Prefix spaces, inverse limits, p-adic residues, and tree level stabilizers are standard. Counting six equivalent descriptions does not create six research results. The cognitive labels do not change those proofs.

**Result:** succeeds against a broad mathematical-novelty claim. M01-M06 supply close theorem-level prior structures. The coordinate-free theorem and universal property improve the draft's precision, but not enough to assert a new general theory.

**Revision:** label the work a correction/synthesis; cite exact topology and tree sources; compress the equivalence theorem; state the sharper adaptive result early, without priority marketing. A short note remains defensible. The hostile case does not invalidate correct mathematics merely because it is standard.

## Attack 2: "The cognitive interpretation is post hoc"

**Strongest hostile case.** Any finite partition makes all its members observationally equivalent. Nothing in the construction selects psychologically meaningful features, their order, the available observer, or a cognitive time scale. A programmer can impose the same quotient on arbitrary objects. Calling a coarser observation unconscious is a label, not evidence.

**Result:** succeeds against an empirical or complete-bi-logic claim, but not against every geometric purpose. Ultrametricity contributes transitive threshold closeness, compatible nested ball partitions and representative-independent cross-distances. The arbitrary-partition triangle counterexample shows why exact distance preservation is not generic coarse-graining.

**Revision:** retain the geometric role; remove any inference of cognitive validation. Primary-source audit distinguishes class membership, relation symmetry, identity, negation, infinity and unfolding. Features, ordering and observation law are external assumptions.

## Attack 3: "The p-adics are decorative"

**Strongest hostile case.** Delete every reference to p-adic numbers. The quotient theorem, completion theorem, adaptive stabilizer theorem and information caution all remain valid. No multiplication, analysis, diffusion, or arithmetic dynamics is used. Therefore the paper cannot be sold primarily as a new p-adic research result.

**Result:** largely succeeds. The canonical residue-ring and inverse-limit specialization is a precise example, not the main contribution. It distinguishes this example from an arbitrary unlabeled tree, but does no essential work in the general proof.

**Revision:** retain one compact specialization explaining Z_p, least-significant digits, reductions modulo p^k and ring structure. Remove suggestions that Q_p or non-Archimedean analysis is required. Do not add decorative operators to compensate.

## Attack 4: "The paper is overstuffed"

**Strongest hostile case.** XOR is unrelated to hierarchical geometry. Martingale convergence is a probability-textbook appendix masquerading as another contribution. A long Paper F bridge blurs the distinction between quotienting and gluing. Cognitive speculation, adaptive coding and inverse limits compete for the title.

**Result:** succeeds against the v0.1 scope. XOR is mathematically correct but does not strengthen this note. Full conditional-expectation machinery adds little once observability is defined. A separate simplicial program has no theorem-level role here.

**Revision:** XOR is removed from the manuscript and preserved as a verified supplementary example. Martingale hypotheses are repaired in the study, not expanded in the body. Paper F gets only a boundary statement. Adaptive symmetry remains because it resolves an actual normality issue; elementary frontier/trie facts move to a short appendix. Information stays only as an exact caution against equating group index and entropy.

## Hostile priority referee

**Strongest hostile case.** The prior audit understates earlier work. Khrennikov 2007 already calls fixed-prefix classes associations. Khrennikov-Yurova 2017 already uses observational quotients with ultrametric latent states. Kleiner 2020 already combines accessible equivalence with automorphism orbits. The current citations on topology and rooted groups are later expositions of still older mathematics. The unlocated exact adaptive normality statement may be an obvious consequence known to specialists.

**Result:** succeeds against the claim that the combined architecture is itself new. It does not prove that every paragraph in the revised note has appeared together, nor that a correction/synthesis is valueless. It leaves a real publication-form question rather than a mathematical contradiction.

**Revision:** cite all three close conceptual predecessors prominently, not buried in a long chronology. Do not say "first unified model," "new resolution geometry," or "new symmetry theory." The exact adaptive criterion is offered with proof and restrained attribution. Source-access gaps are named rather than hidden.

## Specialist attack: "Orbit equivalence silently assumes homogeneity"

**Challenge.** Does the full group really carry any ray of a fiber to any other? Does this survive nonregular branching or a smaller chosen group?

**Resolution.** An explicit independent ray-portrait construction proves transitivity for the full regular subtree. The three-point nonregular counterexample defeats a general claim. Nonregular descendant factors may differ and contain trivial child actions. The manuscript now states the full-group contract each time it matters.

## Specialist attack: "Adaptive quotients need normality"

**Challenge.** Repeated local refinement is not the same as a globally normal stabilizer inclusion. A quotient symbol without a normality check is misleading.

**Resolution.** Theorem 1.1 proves the precise iff criterion. The frontier {00,01,1} gives a counterexample in the binary tree. The index formula survives arbitrary refinement, but a quotient group exists only under the stated per-old-cell full-level condition. This is the strongest mathematical improvement of the revision.

## Specialist attack: "Compactness and radius conventions are being smuggled in"

**Challenge.** Total boundedness alone does not recover the original space from all finite quotients. And open and closed balls at the same numerical radius differ in the prefix metric.

**Resolution.** Proposition 3.1 recovers the completion, with compact X as the special case where nothing is missing. The eventual-zero example demonstrates the gap. Length-k cylinders use closed lambda^k or open lambda^(k-1), and arbitrary thresholds yield bounds for distance, not a false exact equality.

## Specialist attack: "Historical rehabilitation changed the source's meaning"

**Challenge.** The phrase "equivalent without being literally identical" softens Matte-Blanco's stronger language and risks presenting an invented interpretation as a faithful quotation of his theory.

**Resolution.** The primary source audit found the mismatch and v0.2 states the restriction explicitly. The model is not offered as a complete representation of relation-converse symmetry, part/whole identification, altered negation, or conscious unfolding. Exact primary printed-page references replace the broad gloss. Page images still need source-collation in the next gate.

## What the attacks leave standing

The surviving note has a coherent purpose: correct the inference from ultrametric geometry to indistinguishability, give the observation quotient with exact hypotheses, and specify when adaptive symmetry can genuinely be described by a quotient group. It is ready for specialist examination in this narrow form. None of these simulated passes supplies a publication guarantee or an independently certified novel research theorem.
