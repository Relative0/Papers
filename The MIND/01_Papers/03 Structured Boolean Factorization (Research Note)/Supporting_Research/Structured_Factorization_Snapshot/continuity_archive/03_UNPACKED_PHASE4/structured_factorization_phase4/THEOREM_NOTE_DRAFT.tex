\begin{abstract}
We study a structured factorization problem in which a simplicial carrier and a Boolean semantic function must factor across the same partition of attributes.  Building on the fixed-cut correspondence-matrix criterion that conjunctive separability is equivalent to rank at most one over $\F_2$, we isolate two semantic representation classes admitting direct exact extraction and give a persistent SAT fallback for the residual case.  For compressed SDDs we characterize factorability across a vtree split by the presence of at most one nonfalse sub and obtain the finest factorization whose blocks are vtree subtrees.  For support-disjoint read-once operator trees, a signed local CM-rank recursion recovers the full semantic prime AND-factor partition: rank-one essential gates split as signed conjunctions, while rank-two gates fuse their child supports by a four-corner minor argument.  A carrier-block selector encoding realizes all residual four-corner cut tests in one fixed CNF under assumptions.  The results are positioned as a carrier/CM synthesis; SDD canonicity, read-once factorization, and incremental SAT bi-decomposition have substantial prior art.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem}
Let $K$ be a simplicial complex and $f:\{0,1\}^V\to\{0,1\}$.  We seek the finest partition $\Pi$ satisfying
\[
K=*_{B\in\Pi}K|_B,
\qquad
f(x)=\bigwedge_{B\in\Pi}f_B(x_B).
\]
If $\Pi_K$ and $\Pi_f$ are the corresponding prime partitions of the two layers, the simultaneous partition is their least common coarsening.

For a fixed bipartition $A\mid B$, write $[f]_{A\mid B}$ for the Boolean correspondence flattening.  The basic semantic criterion is
\[
f(a,b)=g(a)\land h(b)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{rank}_{\F_2}[f]_{A\mid B}\le 1.
\]

\section{Compressed SDD extraction}
At a vtree split $X\mid Y$, let a compressed SDD node represent
\[
f(X,Y)=\bigvee_{i=1}^m p_i(X)\land s_i(Y),
\]
where the primes form a partition of the $X$ assignments and the subs are pairwise distinct.

\begin{theorem}[Vtree-cut criterion]
For nonfalse $f$, the cut $X\mid Y$ is conjunctively separable if and only if the compressed decision has at most one nonfalse sub.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every assignment $x$ exactly one prime is true, hence the corresponding sub is the right cofactor $f_x(Y)$.  If $f=g(X)\land h(Y)$, every right cofactor is either $0$ or $h$.  Conversely, if all nonzero cofactors are $h$, disjoining the primes associated with $h$ yields $g$.  Compression makes the nonzero $h$ syntactically unique.
\end{proof}

A recursive application yields the finest factor partition whose blocks are vtree-subtree leaf sets.  If the unrestricted prime factors are all vtree subtrees, this recursion is the full prime factorization.

\section{CM rank recursion on read-once operator trees}
Consider a binary operator tree in which each variable appears once and every internal connective is essential in both inputs.  For node $u$ and output polarity $\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$, let $M_u^{(\epsilon)}$ be its $2\times2$ CM, complemented entrywise when $\epsilon=1$.

\begin{theorem}[Signed CM-rank recursion]
Let $u$ have children with disjoint supports $A,B$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $\operatorname{rank}_{\F_2}M_u^{(\epsilon)}=1$, the requested output is a signed conjunction $g^{(\sigma)}\land h^{(\tau)}$ and its prime partition is the union of the recursively computed signed child partitions.
\item If $\operatorname{rank}_{\F_2}M_u^{(\epsilon)}=2$, the requested output has the single prime block $A\cup B$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An essential rank-one binary truth matrix has exactly one $1$, hence is one of the four signed conjunction minterms.  For rank two, every descendant variable is essential.  Fix any $x\in A$, $y\in B$ and assign the other variables so that toggling $x$ toggles the left child and toggling $y$ toggles the right child.  The induced $2\times2$ truth slice is $M_u^{(\epsilon)}$ up to row/column permutation, so it has nonzero determinant.  Any AND factorization separating $x$ and $y$ would force all such minors to vanish.  Thus no cross pair is separable and all of $A\cup B$ is one prime block.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
For this operator-tree class, the exact simultaneous carrier/semantic prime partition is obtained in linear tree time followed by the least-common-coarsening merge with $\Pi_K$.
\end{corollary}

\section{Persistent four-corner SAT fallback}
Introduce one selector per carrier prime block and two assignment clones per variable.  Let mixed-corner inputs be selected by an ITE controlled by the block selector.  One fixed CNF asserts
\[
(F_{00}\land F_{11})\mathbin{\mathsf{XOR}}(F_{01}\land F_{10})=1.
\]
A cut is specified only by selector assumptions.

\begin{theorem}[Assumption equivalence]
For every carrier-compatible cut, the fixed selector-controlled formula under its cut assumptions is satisfiable if and only if the independently rebuilt four-corner defect instance for that cut is satisfiable.
\end{theorem}

This allows direct SDD/CM certificates to split the problem first, with the persistent SAT oracle used only inside unresolved simultaneous blocks.

\section{Claim boundary}
The note does not claim novelty for SDD compression/canonicity, read-once Boolean factorization, or incremental SAT bi-decomposition.  The research question is the exact carrier-constrained simultaneous factorization and the correspondence-matrix certificates connecting these representations.
