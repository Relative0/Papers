The `project/` tree preserves the original folder names. It includes manuscript drafts, TeX sources, referee reports, revisions, research notes, numerical evidence, scripts, logs, receipts, and the existing height-two research ZIP. The `source_archives/` folder holds the original full and core account-transfer ZIPs and earlier ProLT audit/source ZIPs. The transfer ZIPs and their extracted copies are intentionally both present, so you can use this as a single archive without finding the original downloads. The `user_supplied/` folder preserves the two pasted prompts/feedback files from this conversation.

`SHA256_MANIFEST.txt` lists the SHA-256 hash of each payload file. `PACKAGE_RECEIPT.json` beside the download records the completed archive's hash and verification result.

The full transfer's later hostile-audit corrections take precedence over earlier exploratory claims. In particular, preserve the U2 quotient-order repair, U4 mapping-cone terminology, U6 fine-measurability hypothesis, theorem A1, and the bounded RF-IDENT review's scope. The bounded RF-IDENT theorem covers posets with both a least and a greatest element; general height-two RF-IDENT remains open.

Other branches of `The MIND` (such as the separate Unfolding archive, symmetry and consciousness files, and unrelated paper projects) are outside this paper package.
