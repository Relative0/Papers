## Negative / corrective results

- The old compact four-outcome universal protocol does **not** survive literal independent phase tensoring. Each logical outcome leaves 16 distinct Alice phase-pair residual sectors and no Bob-only factorization.
- Several canonical contextuality classes change thousands of individual basis-pair support tables even though the aggregate contextuality hierarchy is unchanged.
- `(0,2)` remains rank 6 and is not universal.
- There is no canonical three-party Choi lift of one shared weighted phase operator. A tested `N^2` leg-weighted GHZ variant differs from the shared-register weighted GHZ behavior.
- A complete 64-effect Bell basis made entirely of transpose-orthogonal 8x8 Boolean matrices is impossible: all such flattened matrices lie in the even-weight hyperplane of dimension 63.
