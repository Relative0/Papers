## What the earlier work already establishes

The baseline has a closed binary subspace/instrument contract, a Frobenius coarse-support encoding, primitive and typed-quotient counterexamples, the algebra and measurement-family normalizer, a Smith filter preorder, the decoder-dependent message counts, and a counterexample to h monotonicity. Its follow-up strengthens the retained-phase obstruction to an iff via a unit minor, identifies the matrix comparison and Sylvester-rank antecedents, and supplies a resolved-disposal activation witness. The nine-page technical companion incorporates those amendments.

Accordingly none of those results is claimed as a newly discovered project theorem in this run. The fresh contribution is a bounded literature recheck, an independently written finite checker and ten regression checks, a rerun of the inherited disposal witness and 31 audit tests, an explicit reconstruction of the matched P01 coding experiment, and a consolidated current evidence/claim ledger. That is valuable verification but not a fourth-paper novelty basis.
