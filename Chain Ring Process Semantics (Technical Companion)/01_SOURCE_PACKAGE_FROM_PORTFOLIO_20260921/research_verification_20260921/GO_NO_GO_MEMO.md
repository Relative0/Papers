## Claim-by-claim publication weight

* Scalar obstruction, primitive conditioning failure and typed-embedding counterexample: essential corrections, elementary; useful companion material.
* Closed subspace/instrument semantics: established field modal theory. It is not a new ring-native physical theory.
* Frobenius support realization: useful exact bridge, but already present locally and built from classical structure. Its nonmonoidal/resource boundary is the contribution in presentation.
* 98,304-element algebra and coarse-measurement stabilizer: self-contained proof and finite construction; elementary specialization with unresolved exact historical attribution. Not enough by itself to anchor a paper.
* Smith selected-filter order: standard subquotient structure and established matrix factorization comparison. It is not a new comparison relation.
* dh, dR and 4dR code counts: distinguish operational access; the new comparisons are orbit-span consequences and P01 supplies dh.
* h/contextuality increase under a filter: a concrete warning against a false monotone, directly using P01.
* Multi-copy retained-phase extraction: exact, but reduces to residue rank and a unit minor over a local ring. No new distillation principle.
* Resolved-disposal activation: already constructed in the earlier review and works on unit sectors; not a nilpotent-only effect. No unresolved existence theorem remains here.
