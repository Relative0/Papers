\begin{abstract}
This note records a fresh verification of the process-semantics comparison already
developed in the project's 20 September technical companion. The unchanged
nonzero-support interpretation over $A=\A$ cannot respect independent scalar
composition. A closed binary-modal completion realizes the original bipartite
support tables through coarse effects, but changes tensor products and conditioning.
We state the precise interface, the selected-filter order and the operation-dependent
multi-copy boundary. The algebraic ingredients have substantial prior art, and no
new historical priority or separate fourth-paper contribution is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and inherited results}
The literal field theory is an instance of modal quantum theory
\cite{SW10,SW12}. Primitivity over rings is an established state-space choice
\cite{DB14}; support contextuality uses the established global-section language
\cite{AB11}. The earlier local companion and restricted-conversion review already
contain the principal propositions below. This note accompanies an independent
finite checker, a primary-source ledger and a reconciliation report. It is not a
replacement for P01, P02 or Paper B.

\begin{proposition}[Independent-success obstruction]
There is no multiplicative predicate $p:A\to\{0,1\}$ with $p(0)=0$ and
$p(a)=1$ for every nonzero $a$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$u^3$ and $u$ are nonzero and $u^3u=0$. Multiplicativity gives $0=1$.
\end{proof}
Primitive vectors are closed under invertible $A$ maps and balanced tensors:
coordinate ideals are preserved and a pair of unit coordinates has a unit product.
But the second Alice coordinate of the primitive resource $\diag(1,u)$ leaves
$(0,u)$ on Bob. The selected nonzero state is not primitive. Furthermore the
abstract tensor $(u^3)\otimes_A(u)$ is isomorphic to $A/(u)$, while the tensor of
its inclusions into $A\otimes_A A$ is zero. Merely retaining a quotient or ideal
type does not justify treating the embeddings as monoidal equivalences.

\section{A closed operational contract}
Systems are finite binary vector spaces $V$, with tensor unit $\F$ and ordinary
field tensor. States are nonzero subspaces $S\leq V$; a pure state is a nonzero
vector. Zero is an impossible branch. Mixture means span, not coherent addition.
A branch is a finite subspace $K\leq\operatorname{Hom}_{\F}(V,W)$, acting by
$S\mapsto\Span\{Ts:T\in K,s\in S\}$. A labelled family $(K_i)$ is complete iff
the common kernel of all its maps is zero. Output spaces may depend on the label.

Terminal effects are subspaces of $V^*$; they are possible if some contraction
is nonzero. A complete terminal measurement spans $V^*$. Selecting an outcome
retains its nonzero image subspace. Forgetting labels takes their span. Discarding
a tensor factor contracts by its entire dual. Classical labels may be copied,
communicated and used to control later instruments. Equivalence in the full
theory is equality of state subspaces. Multiplication by an $A$ unit is generally
detectable: the binary $u$-coefficient effect distinguishes $1$ from $1+u$.

\begin{theorem}[Closure]
These operations close under finite sequential and parallel composition,
adaptive conditioning, mixtures and discard, including with an untouched reference.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose finite bases for each process subspace. Completeness says their stacked
map is injective. Field tensor preserves injectivity; sequential complete stacks
also preserve it. Thus every nonzero correlated input has some nonzero outcome.
Composition takes spans of products of maps, and parallel composition spans of
their tensors; associativity and interchange follow from linear algebra. The
rows of an injective stack span the original input dual. Contracting all output
duals therefore gives the same remote marginal as discarding the original local
system. This also establishes possibilistic no-signalling. Classical erasure is
a span, so cannot cancel two alternatives into zero.
\end{proof}
This is a concrete formulation of established field-modal machinery
\cite{SW12}, not a probability rule. A selected filter $T$ can be completed by an
identity-labelled alternative. No real success probability follows.

\section{Exactly what the ring interface preserves}
Restriction of scalars sends $A$ modules and maps faithfully to binary spaces
and maps. It preserves composition and direct sums. Its lax tensor comparison
is the balanced quotient
\[
 U(M)\otimes_{\F}U(N)\longrightarrow U(M\otimes_A N).
\]
Its unit map is $\F\to U(A)$, $1\mapsto1$. It is not strong monoidal: the unit
dimensions differ, as do the free tensor dimensions $16mn$ and $4mn$. The
comparison kills $u^3\otimes_{\F}u$. An $A$ preparation has source $A$, so after
restriction of scalars it still has source $U(A)$, not the binary tensor unit.

Set $\tau(a)=[u^3]a$ and
\[
 \Omega=\sum_{r=0}^3u^r\otimes_{\F}u^{3-r},\qquad
 \Delta(a)=(a\otimes1)\Omega.
\]
For $C\in\operatorname{Mat}_{m,n}(A)$ define
\[
 E(C)=\sum_{i,j,r}(e_i u^r)\otimes_{\F}(e_j C_{ij}u^{3-r}).
\]
For an $A$ row $x$, let $L_x:U(A^m)\to U(A)$ be its binary block map.

\begin{proposition}[Coarse support realization]
$E$ is injective, intertwines the local $A$-linear actions, and
\[
 (L_x\otimes L_y)E(C)=\Delta(xCy^T).
\]
Consequently coarse measurements made of the block rows of invertible $A$
bases give exactly the original nonzero-$A$ bipartite support tables.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Multiplication by $u$ on either leg of $\Omega$ yields the same sum. By linearity
this holds for every scalar. Also $(\operatorname{id}\otimes\tau)\Delta(a)=a$,
so $\Delta$ is injective. The formula follows blockwise. An invertible $A$ basis
expands to an invertible binary map, so its block-row instrument is complete.
\end{proof}
The coefficient matrix is $\rho(C)J$, with $J$ the invertible block reversal.
For Smith exponents $a_i$, including $a_i=4$ for zeros, its binary rank is
$R=\sum_i(4-a_i)$. In particular $E(1)=\Omega$ has rank four although $1$ is a
balanced product. Discarding one leg of $\Omega$ yields all of $U(A)$ as a mixed
state. Thus neither separability nor the raw shared-ring update is preserved.
Moreover $\mu\Delta=0$, so this Frobenius construction is not a separable
inverse to balancing; separable preservation hypotheses cannot be imported
\cite{MS10}.

\section{Restricted conversion and information tasks}
Fix encoded resources and local selected $A$-linear filters, with no additional
entangled resource or coefficient-resolving disposal. Every successful finite
transcript is a pair of local products $F,G$.

\begin{theorem}[One-copy order]
Pad the ascending source and target Smith lists with exponent four to compatible
sizes. A nonzero target $D$ is obtainable as $FCG^T$ iff $b_i\geq a_i$ for every $i$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The image of $FCG^T$ is a quotient of a submodule of the image of $C$.
For a finite $A$ module with cyclic lengths $\ell_i$, the number of lengths at
least $t$ is the dimension of $\operatorname{soc}(M)\cap u^{t-1}M$. These
subspaces inject under submodule inclusion. For a quotient the layers
$u^{t-1}M/u^tM$ surject; their dimensions give the same counts. Thus cyclic
lengths cannot increase componentwise, which says $b_i\geq a_i$. Conversely
Smith-reduce, select the desired coordinates, multiply them by the required
powers of $u$, and undo target basis changes.
\end{proof}
The underlying factorization comparison is established
\cite{APGPS,Antoine}. It must be distinguished from the larger Malcolmson
order, which admits an additional triangular-block erasure \cite{HungLi}.
Normalized quotient-regular ranks are established Sylvester ranks \cite{JL}.
They need not be a complete family of monotone inequalities for pure filtering.

The local binary algebra normalizer and the coarse measurement stabilizer are
both $\GL_2(A)\rtimes\operatorname{Aut}_{\F}(A)$, of order $98,304$; their
action on the 24 projective points has order $12,288$. The complete center,
pointwise-stabilizer and dual-action proofs are in the accompanying theorem
ledger and the earlier local review. This is a proved inherited result, not an
open claim in this note.

Leading multiplicity $h$ is not a filter monotone:
\[
 \diag(u,1)\diag(1,u)=\diag(u,u).
\]
Here $h$ increases from one to two and P01's fixed-family contextuality becomes
strong, while binary rank falls from seven to six. For local dimension $d\geq2$,
the exact message counts are $dh$ for the matched P01 decoder, $dR$ for the
same encoders with arbitrary binary decoding, and $4dR$ with full binary local
encoding. These are different access contracts, not three capacities of one task.

\section{Independent copies and disposal}
Independent copies carry distinct scalar variables,
$B_k=\F[u_1,\ldots,u_k]/(u_i^4)$. Let $r_0$ be the number of unit Smith factors.
With all phase registers retained and $B_k$-linear selected filters, extraction
of $E_{B_k}(I_d)$ is possible iff $d\leq r_0^k$. Indeed reduction to the residue
field gives the necessary rank bound; selecting a unit minor and multiplying
by its inverse proves sufficiency. This is elementary local-ring algebra,
not a new activation principle.

Resolved disposal changes the result. With $v=u_2$, take
\[
 N=\diag(1,v^2,u_1^2,u_1^2v^2),\quad
 L=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&0\\v&0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}v^3&0&0&0\\v^2&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Then $LNQ^T=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}v^3&v^2\\0&v^3\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
Selecting $[v^3]$ on each phase leg gives $E_A(I_2)$. Forgetting those labels
instead yields the two-dimensional span of $E_A(I_2)$ and $E_A(E_{12})$.
This inherited witness was rerun exactly. It uses only the unit sector, so
does not isolate a resource unique to nilpotent amplitudes.

\section{Evidence and disposition}
The new checker covers all $65,536$ two-by-two $A$ matrices, $1,966,080$
single-sided filter cases, $8,640$ representative coarse-effect pairs and
$98,304$ distinct constructed normalizer elements. Ten new regression tests
and 31 canonical audit tests pass. None of these is an exhaustive scan of
$\GL(8,2)$ or a proof of historical priority. The old audit manifest has nine
pre-existing mismatches; they are recorded without altering its data.

The principal results already occur in the local technical companion, and the
fresh literature check reinforces their classical antecedents. The correct
disposition is a verification supplement, not a separate fourth full paper.
General pure-target conversion with unobserved phase disposal remains open
only after a precise operation class is motivated. There is no claimed Born
rule, physical implementation, speedup or undeclared communication advantage.
