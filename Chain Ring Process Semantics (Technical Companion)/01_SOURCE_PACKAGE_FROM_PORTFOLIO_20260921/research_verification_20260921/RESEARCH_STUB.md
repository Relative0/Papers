# Smallest useful remaining research problem

The existence of a closed literal binary completion is settled. A faithful Boolean nonzero interpretation with all shared-A scalar events and balanced independent composition is impossible. The exact retained-phase free-target criterion and the existence of resolved-disposal activation are also settled. They are not missing publication milestones.

A potentially useful remaining theorem would characterize pure-target extraction when allowed collective maps respect all phase actions before **unobserved** disposal of specified phase registers, with no postselection on the erased coefficient outcomes and no entangled ancillary resource. The operation class must be justified independently of the desire for a theorem. Local logical ancillas, reference preservation, selected other outcomes and whether repeated rounds may change the phase labels must be fixed first.

The smallest concrete test case is two copies of E_A(diag(1,u^2)), target E_A(I_2), with one phase register retained. The known selected-coefficient witness succeeds, but its unobserved output is span{E_A(I_2),E_A(E_12)}. That does not prove impossibility for all allowed filters. A necessary-and-sufficient criterion, with a proof of closure of its declared process class, would change the technical research assessment. It would change the full-paper decision only after a specialist priority comparison and a demonstration that it is not another ordinary rank/factorization corollary.

Alternatively a ring-native operational completion may change the tensor, scalar possibility, effect rules or state class. It must say exactly which assumption of the scalar obstruction is relaxed and reprove independent preparation and conditional composition; quotient labels alone do not suffice.

No research action remains required for this dossier. Preserve the existing companion and this verification snapshot. Resume only when the phase-access contract has an operational motivation.
