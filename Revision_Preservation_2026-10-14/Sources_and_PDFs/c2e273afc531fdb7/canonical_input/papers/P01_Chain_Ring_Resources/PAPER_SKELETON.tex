\begin{abstract}
We study possible-outcome support for bipartite matrices over finite commutative
chain rings, using explicitly specified complete local basis measurements.
The proposed article centers on a classification by the number of nonzero
Smith factors and the multiplicity of the least valuation. Under a matched
invertible-orbit communication task, the latter invariant gives an exact
$d h$-message codebook bound. Universal exact teleportation instead requires
invertibility and an admitted complete analyzer, giving sharp resource
separations. We compare shared-ring and literal field tensors, explain the
collapse of restricted distinctions under enlarged operations, and give a
chosen symbolic-support interface back to Boolean logical matrices. These
results concern algebraic support, not Born probabilities or a closed
unrestricted process theory. The final introduction must distinguish candidate
new classification/synthesis from standard ring and modal ingredients after
the remaining theorem-level priority review.
\end{abstract}
