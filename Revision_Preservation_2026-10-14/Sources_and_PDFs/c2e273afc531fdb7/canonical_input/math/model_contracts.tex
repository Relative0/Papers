\section{Operational contracts}
\label{sec:contracts}
\paragraph{F: field modal model.}
States are nonzero vectors of $\F_2^d$, composite spaces use $\otimes_{\F_2}$,
and the allowed reversible group is stated explicitly. An unrestricted complete
readout is a dual basis; an outcome is possible exactly when its coefficient is
nonzero. No real probabilities or positive inner product are supplied.

\paragraph{S: shared-ring model.}
Local carriers are $K^d$, composites use $\otimes_K$, and complete local
measurements are primitive projective bases. Static resource tensors are
nonzero. This is not yet a closed all-nonzero preparation theory: nonzero tensor
factors can annihilate. A primitive-only preparation restriction repairs that
problem but is not closed under the specified conditioning rule.

\paragraph{L: literal registers.}
A logical-plus-phase carrier is $\F_2^2\otimes_{\F_2}\F_2^4=\F_2^8$.
Its $n$ independent copies have dimension $8^n$, whereas the binary dimension of
$(\A^2)^{\otimes_\A n}$ is $4\cdot2^n$. Literal restricted coarse readouts and
unrestricted $\GL_8(\F_2)$ readouts are different contracts. The regular
representation is a faithful matrix-algebra map, not a monoidal equivalence of
these state spaces.

\paragraph{DC: exact fixed-orbit codebooks.}
For $M\in\Mat_d(K)\setminus\{0\}$, $d\geq2$, Alice encodes by
$U\in\GL_d(K)$, giving $UM$. Bob has one arbitrary complete invertible
$K$-linear basis readout on the $d^2$ joint coefficients. Every possible outcome
must identify the message. Discarding the fixed resource and preparing a fresh
carrier is outside this orbit count.

\paragraph{TP: exact universal rank-one teleportation.}
For every nonzero input, including an arbitrary untouched reference system,
all possible branches of a complete joint rank-one analyzer must be corrected
exactly. In a fixed vectorization convention the transfer is $T_m=M^T E_m^T$.
A complete basis of flattened $E_m$ and invertibility of every reshape $E_m$
are separate requirements. Allowed corrections must contain the necessary
inverses. No unrecorded failures or input-dependent environment may be ignored.

\paragraph{QXOR and its lower-bound extension.}
The standard oracle is
$U_f\lvert x,y\rangle=\lvert x,y+f(x)\rangle$ over a characteristic-two field.
Arbitrary finite ancillas and oracle-independent linear processing are allowed.
Complete adaptive instruments can be recorded in a direct sum; the collected
readout is injective and every possible outcome must be correct. Classical
inspection of a state description or its complete support is not free readout.
The lower bounds extend to
$O_f=\sum_x\lvert x\rangle\langle x\rvert\otimes G_{f(x)}$ for fixed invertible
$G_0,G_1$. Matching upper bounds are asserted for QXOR, not arbitrary
$G_0,G_1$: the choice $G_0=G_1$ makes all functions indistinguishable.

\paragraph{PROB: additional real completion problem.}
A real probability completion is nonnegative, normalized and no-signalling.
It is faithful only if every supported event has positive probability. A weak
completion can delete possible events by assigning them zero. Neither type is
inferred automatically from modal support.
