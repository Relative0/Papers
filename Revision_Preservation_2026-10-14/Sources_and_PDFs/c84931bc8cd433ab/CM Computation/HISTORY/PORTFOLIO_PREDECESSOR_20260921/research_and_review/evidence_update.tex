\subsection{Exploratory mechanism and generic ties}
\label{sec:mechanism-evidence}

The canonical S1/S2 record contains 10,500 generated cases, of which 10,389
completed in all four arms: direct ANF, sharing-aware common-subexpression
evaluation, generic local truth-function optimization, and CM folding. The
all-arm population consists of 3,750 S1 and 6,639 S2 cases. The remaining 111
cases are retained with their budget patterns: 108 fail in all arms, two
complete only in the generic and CM arms, and one completes only in the
sharing arm. No completed correctness disagreement was recorded.

CM and generic optimization agree on seven metrics in every all-arm admitted
case: final degree, final monomial count, multiplication count, output unique
nodes, pair terms, peak monomials and serialized bytes. The generic arm records
more folds on 6,010 cases and CM more on none; fold counts are not timings.
For corrected S2, median direct/CM multiplication counts at requested sharing
fractions $0$, $0.25$ and $0.5$ are respectively $35/35$, $17/12$ and $9/0$.
These strata exhibit both nonhelp and pre-expansion savings. The generic arm
achieves the same reductions. An earlier generator's support defect is
excluded from this corrected population.

This evidence supports the mechanism of simplifying a small live function
before polynomial expansion. It does not establish a CM-specific semantic
reduction or confirmatory performance advantage. A fresh independent recount
of the raw records reproduced the admission counts and all seven ties; the
supplement records the raw-file hash and the recount output.

\subsection{A completed negative dispatcher study: P14-PY0}
\label{sec:p14}

Newer local records, completed after the consolidation snapshot, provide a
frozen comparison of recipe-to-owned-ANF compilation. Direct lowering (D)
builds an ordinary source DAG and lowers it. The minimal P13 observer (F)
records dispatch information during source construction. Exact dispatch (E)
uses a bounded exact decision and either lowers the original DAG directly or
constructs folding metadata for a selected case. Always-fold (A) and a generic
signature optimizer (G) are diagnostic comparators. The production timer ends
before output equality checks and reporting; preparation needed by each arm
is included. The separate compiler-IR endpoint starts with an existing root
and charges any observer clone. D here is a symbolic ANF lowerer, not the
packed truth-function arm of protocol v3.

The frozen local campaign used 384 synthetic cases from 12 families and 107
natural cones from 14 previously uninspected EPFL design families. It retained
three fresh sequential workers, each with three measured blocks after one
warm-up, for 51,102 measured rows including secondary and canonicalization
arms. Equality checks passed across all 491 frozen cases and 512 targeted
correctness cases. E selected folding on five synthetic cases and no natural
cone. These counts describe this acquired corpus, not prevalence in general
software or circuits.

The frozen primary rule required E/F geometric mean at most $0.99$, E/D at
most $0.95$, and the relevant simultaneous upper bound below one on both
corpora. It did not pass. At the recipe endpoint E was about 5.1\% slower
than D on synthetic cases and 6.1\% slower on natural cones. E improved on F,
but the natural E/F simultaneous interval included parity. The five selected
synthetic cases had E/D ratios between $0.075$ and $0.389$; their local benefit
did not offset the aggregate policy boundary. The synthetic ratio of summed
case medians was $0.9899$, whereas the preregistered case-balanced geometric
mean was $1.0508$. We retain the latter decision rule rather than selecting
the favorable aggregation after seeing the data.

The local freeze reduced the draft design to three workers and 10 ms target
batches with a predicted 40 ms slow-arm cap; 14 eligible natural families
replaced the draft target of 16. The host ran Python 3.10.11 on Windows with
Balanced power and uncontrolled affinity. The freeze treats close effects as
inconclusive. Disclosed amendments corrected a per-arm correctness timeout,
extended wall-time caps, and accelerated analysis by indexing rows without
changing the resampling order or statistic. Original and amended freezes
are retained. A separate 2,455-observation memory pass used
\texttt{tracemalloc}; it measures traced allocation peaks, not process RSS.
Its E/D geometric-mean peak ratios were $1.0931$ synthetic and $1.0065$ natural,
and are descriptive rather than confirmatory intervals.

For this revision, read-only reconciliation verified all 15 entries of the
run's artifact manifest, all case ratios in 17 recorded comparisons, and the
four primary 10,000-draw bootstrap intervals directly from the raw timing
rows. This is fresh verification of an inherited completed run, not a new
timing campaign. The decision remains negative for the unchanged dispatcher.
The subsequent P15 late-scan prototype has exposed-case correctness records
but no performance timings or fresh holdout; it does not replace this result.
